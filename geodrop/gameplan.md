# Google Ads Dropshipping Gameplan

Source: YouTube transcript (2026)
Creator: 15 brands, 8-figure portfolio, $1k/day per store

---

## Core Thesis

**Sell saturated products in unsaturated markets.**

Saturated = proven demand, people actively spending money.
Unaturated = Google search, where nobody else is advertising.

Let other people on Facebook/TikTok/Instagram create demand. We capture it on Google for a fraction of the cost.

## Product Selection

**Source 1:** AliExpress — sort by orders in your niche
**Source 2:** Meta Ads Library — find winning Facebook/TikTok creatives
**Source 3:** Spy tools (Kalodata, WinningHunter, Minea, PiPiAds)
**Source 4:** Google — find top 3 competitors, combine best elements
**Source 5:** ChatGPT — "What are the top 50 bestselling products in [niche]?"

**Validation:** Google Ads Keyword Planner
- Set to target countries (US, CA, AU, NZ, UK)
- Look for: 10K+ monthly searches, reasonable CPC ($0.50-$3)
- Volume above 10K = add to store. Ideally 100K+.

---

## Pricing Psychology (Google vs Facebook)

**Facebook:** Pulling someone out of entertainment mode → hard to sell $100+
**Google:** Already in buying mode → $100+ easy

Tested across 15 stores:
- Product at $39.99 → 2.5% conversion
- Same product at $79.99 → **5% conversion**
- Double price = double conversion = 4x revenue per visitor

**Target AOV: $100+** (only need 10 orders/day for $1k)

- Clean and professional (not perfect)
- Collections-based (not one-product)
- AI-generated with ChatGPT + Atlas.ai
- Build in one day
- AOV boosters: bundles, urgency, upsells, authority signals

Example: Namoya has 50 beauty products, all dropshipped, $200k/month profit.

## Google Ads Scaling

**Campaign type:** Performance Max (PMax) = Google's AI

PMax shows your products across:
- Shopping tab
- Gmail
- Search
- YouTube ads (AI-generated!)
- Discover
- Display ads
- Google Maps

**Scaling formula:**
```
Start: $50/day
↓ (profitable)
$100/day
↓ (profitable)
$200-250/day
↓ (profitable)
$400/day
↓
repeat
```

Every stage: wait until insanely profitable → reinvest profits.

## Product Expansion

Alongside budget scaling, expand collections:
- Heated jacket works → add heated vest, sherpa, gloves, beanie
- More products = more data = Google learns faster = warmer buyers
- Google wants to make you money. Feed it products.

More products = more entry points = more chances to click and buy.

Stores run 1,000-2,000 products. Some have 10,000+ SKUs.

## Google Merchant Center Approval

## Key Principles

1. **Don't chase $1k/day spikes** — build $1k/day every day you're running
2. **Sell validated products** — not cold experiments
3. **Let others create demand** — capture on Google
4. **Higher AOV = less headache** — $100 AOV > $50 AOV
5. **More products = more data = more sales** — feed Google constantly
6. **Automate everything** — listing, research, operations
7. **Exit potential** — stores sell for 2-3x annual profit
