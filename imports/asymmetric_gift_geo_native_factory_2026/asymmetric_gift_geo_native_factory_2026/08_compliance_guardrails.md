# Compliance + Product Guardrails

## 1. Do not “turn existing self-help books into ebooks”

The safe and commercially better approach is:
- identify the **problem/search intent**,
- research multiple independent sources,
- use public-domain facts, original analysis, and your own framework,
- write an original product from scratch.

Do not reproduce an author's wording, examples, chapter structure, distinctive exercises or illustrations.

Amazon KDP currently requires AI-generated-content disclosure and specifically restricts certain companion books based on copyrighted works.

## 2. Etsy AI policy

Etsy currently allows seller-prompted AI creations when the seller has a real creative role, but requires AI-use disclosure. Production partners also need disclosure.

Operational implication:
- curate outputs,
- keep original house styles,
- show representative finished examples,
- disclose AI assistance,
- do not pretend machine output is hand-painted.

## 3. Likeness and privacy

For personalized gifts:
- buyer should confirm they have permission/right to upload photos where appropriate,
- keep private artifacts unlisted by default,
- allow deletion,
- avoid training on customer photos without explicit consent,
- never imply celebrity endorsement.

## 4. High-stakes decision products

Renovation, relocation, landlord, financial and similar products should be framed as:
- organization,
- document comparison,
- question generation,
- planning support.

Do not claim:
- professional engineering approval,
- legal advice,
- immigration approval,
- tax advice,
- guaranteed savings/outcomes.

Where law/regulation matters, link to current official sources and timestamp the information.

## 5. Personalized songs / art

Use:
- original melodies/styles,
- licensed audio assets,
- user-owned photos,
- original house visual styles.

Avoid:
- “make it exactly like [living artist]”
- copyrighted characters unless licensed
- cloned celebrity voices
- copied lyrics.

## 6. Reviews

Do not fabricate reviews or sales counters. Early trust should come from:
- free/discounted beta customers with honest feedback,
- real examples,
- clear preview,
- money-back/revision policy where operationally feasible.

## 7. The best anti-slop rule

Every product should contain at least one element that could not have been generated without **that buyer's data**.

If the output could be sold unchanged to 1,000 people, it is probably becoming a commodity.
