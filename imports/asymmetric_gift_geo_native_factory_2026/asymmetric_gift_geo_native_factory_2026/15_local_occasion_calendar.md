## SKU formula
`market × occasion × recipient × artifact`

Example:
Germany × Christmas × Dad × Newspaper
Germany × Christmas × Dad × Crossword
Germany × Christmas × Mum × Comic

One market can support hundreds of pages without hundreds of product systems.

## Search-first rule
Before launching a SKU:
1. search exact native phrase,
2. inspect Google Shopping / Etsy / local marketplace,
3. note price and review counts,
4. inspect weak incumbent UX,
5. create a materially better finished artifact,
6. test with a tiny budget.
