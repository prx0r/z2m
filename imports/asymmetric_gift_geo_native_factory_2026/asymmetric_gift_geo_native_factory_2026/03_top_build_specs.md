# Top Build Specs


## 1. Relationship Mini-Comic

**One-line promise:** Turn 10 memories + 8 photos into a polished 20–30 page comic gift.

**Inputs**
- recipient names
- relationship/occasion
- 8–15 memories or inside jokes
- 5–12 photos
- tone/style choices

**Outputs**
- cover + 20–30 pages
- shareable web preview
- print-ready PDF
- optional hardcover/POD order

**Price ladder:** $39 digital / $69 print / $99 deluxe

**Best launch channels:** Etsy first; direct SEO pages for anniversary, Christmas, wedding, Valentine's

**Why AI changes the economics**
LLM structures story; image model generates scenes; layout engine composes pages. One workflow can serve couples, parents, friends, retirement and pets.

**Q4 angle:** “Looks like you worked on it for weeks.” Last-minute digital delivery with physical copy following later.

**Guardrail:** Do not imitate living artists or copyrighted characters; use original house styles.


## 2. One-Afternoon Family Memoir

**One-line promise:** Record a relative for 60–90 minutes and receive a designed keepsake book.

**Inputs**
- voice/audio interview
- name + dates
- photos
- family member list
- optional timeline

**Outputs**
- edited narrative chapters
- timeline
- quote pullouts
- photo captions
- PDF + hardcover-ready file

**Price ladder:** $59 digital / $99 hardcover bundle / $149 multiple-copy family bundle

**Best launch channels:** DTC Google/Meta; Christmas and Mother's/Father's Day

**Why AI changes the economics**
Speech-to-text + LLM editing collapses what used to be weeks of editorial work. Storyworth/Remento validate price and gifting behavior.

**Q4 angle:** Christmas gift for parents/grandparents; sell the *capture session* rather than a year-long subscription.

**Guardrail:** User must review names/dates; avoid inventing family facts.


## 3. Private Crossword / Trivia Gift

**One-line promise:** Give us the people, places and jokes; get a real playable puzzle about them.

**Inputs**
- 10–20 answers/memories
- occasion
- difficulty
- tone
- recipient name

**Outputs**
- crossword + answer key
- optional trivia round
- printable PDF
- mobile share link

**Price ladder:** $9 single / $19 deluxe / $29 party pack

**Best launch channels:** Etsy; direct occasion landing pages; office/retirement B2B

**Why AI changes the economics**
Grid generation is deterministic; AI writes/refines clues. Near-zero marginal cost, instant delivery, extremely legible gift.

**Q4 angle:** Secret Santa, Christmas dinner, office party, New Year's.

**Guardrail:** Keep a human-review step for clue ambiguity and offensive/private content.

**Outputs**

**Q4 angle:** Christmas is ideal: instant digital fallback + physical card follows.

**Guardrail:** Moonpig already has strong AI/personalization; do not compete on generic card creation alone.
