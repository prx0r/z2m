# Low-Capital Validation Recipes

## Rule
Spend capital proving demand, not production. Digital delivery is near-zero fulfilment cost; POD removes inventory; marketplaces supply initial demand; AI collapses personalization cost.

## 1. Marketplace → DTC
Launch 5–10 Etsy listings around one artifact, each aimed at a different recipient/occasion. Fulfil digitally. Only build a dedicated local site for listings that get impressions, favorites and orders.

## 2. Search-intent parasite
Best for expensive decisions. Target queries immediately before someone spends serious money:
- kitchen renovation quote comparison
- wedding venue comparison
- moving abroad checklist
- golf simulator room size
- Airbnb welcome book

Ad promise: “Upload your 3 quotes. Get one clean comparison.”

## 3. Static template → done-for-you
Find a template category with visible sales. Replace “download this spreadsheet” with “upload your real documents; we complete it for you.”

Examples:
- wedding spreadsheet → vendor comparison report
- renovation spreadsheet → quote auditor
- Airbnb template → completed guest guide
- golf planner → scorecard-based plan

## 4. Digital first → POD after purchase
Generate digital artifact → buyer approves → offer print upgrade → place POD order. No stock, no warehouse, no dead seasonal inventory.

## 5. Last-minute arbitrage
After physical shipping cutoffs, change creative to:
**“A genuinely personal gift in 10 minutes.”**
Best: crossword, newspaper, song, comic, trivia, digital yearbook.

## 6. Country-by-country SEO fork
Create exact native pages rather than one broad global page. Local examples and local FAQ matter more than literal translation.

## 7. Local ritual arbitrage
Global competitors optimize generic Christmas/birthday/wedding. Attack:
- Germany: Einschulung / Schultüte
- Netherlands: Sinterklaas
- Denmark/Norway: confirmation
- Sweden: student graduation
- Spain/Italy: communion
- France: family/grandparent occasions

## 8. B2B channel
Repackage the same engine for retirements, employee milestones, real-estate closing gifts, photographers, pet businesses and golf coaches. One buyer can create recurring orders without repeated consumer CAC.

## 9. White-label local sellers
Give local Etsy/Instagram sellers the generator. They supply language, taste and customer trust; you supply rendering/fulfilment. Charge per artifact or wholesale.

## 10. Review compounder
First 50 reviews matter more than first 50 features. Optimize preview quality, turnaround, one revision and honest delivery.

## Validation budget per country
- Stage 0: €0–€20 — keyword scan, marketplace scan, 5 examples, landing page.
- Stage 1: €50 — one artifact, 3–5 angles, digital-only delivery.
- Stage 2: +€100 only if starts/checkouts appear — price test and local checkout.
- Stage 3: €250–€500 only after real sales — POD, retargeting, SEO expansion.

Do not spend upfront on apps, printers, warehouses, elaborate brand systems, giant content sites or country-specific entities. Unlock investment with **paid orders**.
