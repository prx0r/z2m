# Marketplace Playbook

## Etsy

Best for:
- obvious gift artifacts
- personalized digital downloads
- POD add-ons
- recipient + occasion keyword combinations

Winning format:
`[artifact] + [recipient] + [occasion] + [personalization hook]`

Example:
**Custom Crossword for Boyfriend, Personalized Anniversary Gift, Our Story Puzzle, Instant Digital Download**

Important current policy:
- seller-prompted AI creations are allowed,
- AI use must be disclosed,
- production partners must be disclosed,
- finished product should be represented accurately.

Do not sell prompt bundles as the product.

## Direct Shopify / lightweight checkout

Best for:
- document upload
- voice/photo intake
- interactive preview
- £29–£99 decision products
- higher AOV
- Google Search Ads

Use exact-intent landing pages:
- `/kitchen-quote-comparison`
- `/moving-uk-to-portugal`
- `/break-90-golf-plan`
- `/airbnb-welcome-guide-generator`

## Google Search

Best for expensive-decision products.

Keyword structure:
- `[problem] checklist`
- `[decision] comparison`
- `[thing] quote comparison`
- `[thing] planner`
- `[thing] cost spreadsheet`
- `moving from [A] to [B] checklist`
- `how to choose [vendor]`
- `what to ask [contractor]`

Ad promise should be specific:
> Upload your 3 kitchen quotes. Get one normalized comparison in minutes.

## Amazon KDP

Use for:
- static evergreen niche guides
- workbook versions
- physical companion products

Do **not** build the core personalized workflow here.

Current KDP policy requires disclosure of AI-generated content. KDP also warns about companion books based on copyrighted works; do not turn a popular self-help book into a near-derivative “summary” product.

## Not On The High Street

Potentially useful for premium physical/personalized versions once the product has proof elsewhere. Personalized/bespoke goods fit the marketplace's gifting orientation, but validate unit economics and seller acceptance before investing.

## Corporate / B2B

Best products:
- retirement tribute books
- employee milestone books
- team farewell newspapers
- collaborative cards + memory artifacts
- client-property handover manuals

One HR/admin buyer can create repeat occasions.

## The marketplace transmutation method

For every backend engine, create:
1. one broad DTC product,
2. 10–30 narrow Etsy listings,
3. 5–20 SEO pages,
4. 3–10 Google exact-intent ad groups,
5. one premium physical/POD option,
6. one group/corporate variant.

Do not create a new backend until an existing engine cannot serve the new use case.
