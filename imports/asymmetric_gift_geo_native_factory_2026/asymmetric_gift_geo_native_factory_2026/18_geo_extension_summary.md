# Geo-Native Extension — Executive Summary

## New thesis
Original:
> AI makes personalized gifts and high-value decision reports cheap to produce.

Extension:
> Reuse the same engine in wealthy non-English markets where native language, native checkout, local occasions and local examples create a conversion moat.

## Best first five
1. Switzerland — premium digital + TWINT + multilingual
2. Germany — scale + local rituals + high-intent decision products
3. Norway — wealthy small language + Vipps
4. Netherlands — 94% online-shopping penetration + iDEAL + card culture
5. Sweden — high digital adoption + Swish

## Repeatable business motions
1. Marketplace → DTC.
2. Static template → done-for-you.
3. Digital first → POD after payment.
4. Expensive decision → cheap certainty.
5. English winner → native-query fork.
6. Generic holiday → local cultural ritual.
7. Consumer artifact → B2B white label.
8. One engine → many recipient/occasion pages.
9. Shipping cutoff → instant-digital campaign.
10. Local seller partnership → enter new language without a local team.

## Capital strategy
Treat each country as a **€50–€150 experiment** until paid orders appear.

Do not buy inventory, apps, printers, large campaigns or separate infrastructure before demand proof.

A failed market should be cheap. A winning one should be trivial to scale.
