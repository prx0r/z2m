# Transmutation Recipes

The objective is to build **engines**, not individual products.

## Recipe A — The Recipient Artifact

**Formula:**  
`[recipient relationship] + [occasion] + [private facts/photos] → [recognizable artifact]`

Artifacts:
- crossword
- newspaper
- comic
- storybook
- magazine
- quiz
- coloring book
- song
- map
- yearbook
- trading cards
- awards booklet

Examples:
- boyfriend + anniversary + memories → relationship comic
- dad + Christmas + family facts → “Dad Times” newspaper
- coworker + retirement + colleague stories → tribute magazine
- dog owner + Christmas + pet photos → pet adventure book

**Why it works:** recipient-specific input creates perceived craftsmanship. The buyer cannot get the same product from a shelf.

**SKU multiplication:**  
`10 artifacts × 8 recipients × 8 occasions = 640 landing/listing combinations`  
You do not need 640 codebases.

## Recipe B — The Expensive-Decision Parasite

**Formula:**  
`[£5k–£100k decision] × [confusion] → £29–£79 decision-support product`

Examples:
- kitchen renovation → quote comparison
- wedding → venue/vendor comparison
- international relocation → move blueprint
- golf simulator → room + budget build plan
- first rental property → operating pack
- classic-car restoration → project binder

**Rule:** The digital product should cost **<0.5% of the underlying transaction** whenever possible. At that point price resistance falls sharply if the product plausibly prevents one mistake.

**Do not sell generic information.** Ingest the buyer's actual quote, room, budget, scorecard, listing or document.

## Recipe C — Static Template → Personalized Service

Find an Etsy/marketplace template with thousands of reviews.

Transform:
`template → questionnaire/upload → generated completed artifact`

Examples:
- renovation spreadsheet → quote auditor
- wedding spreadsheet → vendor comparator
- Airbnb welcome-book template → complete property guide
- golf planner → personalized practice program
- RV checklist → model/trip-specific command cards

**Signal:** a crude template with 1k+ reviews means customers are already paying for organization. Personal completion is the premium.

---

## Recipe D — Digital First, Physical Second

1. Sell instant PDF/web result.
2. Upsell print/POD.
3. Upsell extra copies.
4. Upsell framed/large/premium format.
5. Upsell annual refresh.

This avoids inventory and gives last-minute buyers an immediate gift even if physical shipping misses the date.

---

## Recipe E — Card as Wrapper

The card itself is commodity. Use it to package:
- private crossword
- custom mini comic
- personal song
- memory reel
- relationship quiz
- interactive scavenger hunt
- “open when…” pages
- family timeline

Front of card communicates the hook; QR opens the richer gift.

This changes the comparison from “£3.99 card vs £2 card” to “£14.99 personal experience vs generic card.”

- wedding
- engagement

## Recipe G — Occasion × Recipient × Identity

Marketplace keyword formula:

`[occasion] gift for [recipient] who loves [identity/hobby]`

Examples:
- Christmas gift for golfer dad
- retirement gift for teacher
- anniversary gift for long-distance boyfriend
- new-home gift for first-time buyers
- gift for dog mom who lost her pet

Use the same backend, but the thumbnail and first sentence must make the niche obvious.

## Recipe I — Collaborative Gift

`one buyer → invite 5–50 contributors → AI edits → one artifact`

Products:
- retirement book
- wedding advice book
- 50th birthday memories
- teacher appreciation book
- grandparent family history
- team farewell magazine

The coordination layer is the value. AI makes chaotic submissions coherent.

---

## Recipe J — Premium Proof Ladder

Do not hide everything behind checkout.

Free preview:
- cover
- 3 completed clues
- 1 comic page
- one normalized quote section
- one “before/after” page
- watermarked first spread

Then charge to unlock full result. This makes AI output feel tangible before payment and reduces refund risk.

---

## Recipe K — Review Flywheel

Early moat:
1. niche-specific listing
2. fast turnaround
3. preview
4. buyer can request one revision
5. ask for photo/video review after delivery
6. display real examples
7. clone winning backend into adjacent recipient/occasion pages

The model is replaceable. **Reviews + examples + search rank + conversion history are not.**
