## Landing-page formula
**[Finished artifact], made from [simple input], delivered in [clear time].**

Examples:
- Upload 3 renovation quotes. Get one clean comparison.
- Give us 12 memories. Get a crossword only they can solve.
- Upload old recipe cards. Get your family cookbook.

Page order:
finished example → 3-step process → preview → price → local payment logos → delivery promise → reviews → privacy/deletion promise → one CTA.

Do not lead with “AI”. AI is production infrastructure, not customer value.
