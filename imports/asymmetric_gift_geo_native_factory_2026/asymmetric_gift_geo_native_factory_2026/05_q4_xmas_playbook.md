# Q4 / Christmas Playbook

## Core Q4 thesis

Christmas is not the time to sell abstract software. Sell **instantly understandable gifts**.

The highest-converting promise is:
> **Give us a few facts/photos. We turn them into something only this person could receive.**

Google's 2025 Holiday 100 notes that most US holiday shoppers find choosing the right gift difficult. Etsy's holiday trend reporting emphasized personal expression, nostalgia and gifts that tell a story. Those signals strongly favor personalized artifacts.

## The “forgotten gift” wedge

Digital delivery is a feature, not a compromise.

Landing-page angle:
- “Christmas gift in 10 minutes that doesn’t look last-minute”
- “Made from your memories, not picked from a catalogue”
- “Preview it before you pay”
- “Instant digital version; printed copy can follow”

## Creative principle

A winning marketplace thumbnail should communicate:
1. who it is for,
2. what the finished object is,
3. what makes it personal.

Bad: “AI Personalized Memory Experience”
Good: “A 20-page comic made from your relationship”

## Giftable decision products

Some practical products can be gifts:
- Golf Break-90 plan
- new-puppy plan
- new-home operating manual
- moving-abroad blueprint
- RV trip/setup kit

Phrase them as **“I bought you the thing that makes your expensive hobby/project easier.”**
