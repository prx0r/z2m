# Moonpig Disruption: What Is Actually Attackable?

## First: do not underestimate Moonpig

FY26 facts:
- £373.0m group revenue
- £203.5m Moonpig/Greetz card revenue
- 12.3m active customers
- 36m orders
- £9.32 AOV
- 17.9% gift attach rate
- UK standard card £3.99
- tracked delivery on >40% of UK card-only orders
- AI writing, AI stickers, face swap, voice/video QR cards, group cards, reminders

Moonpig itself says it has ~70% UK online card share versus ~12% for the nearest competitor (source cited by Moonpig: OC&C, Oct 2024).

**Conclusion:** “AI makes cards” is not a wedge. Moonpig already uses AI and owns reminders, delivery, brand and repeat behavior.

## The real wedge: Artifact Cards

A normal card says:
> Happy birthday.

An Artifact Card says:
> I made you a crossword where every answer is about us.

or:
> Scan this: your life has been turned into an eight-page newspaper.

or:
> Scan this to hear the song made from our story.

The physical card is **packaging + ritual + delivery**. The digital artifact is the differentiated gift.

## Unit-economics clue

Moonpig standard card: £3.99.  
Prodigi surfaced wholesale card pricing: roughly £0.75–£1.10 before tax/shipping.

That does *not* mean you automatically have a great business—the acquisition and postage costs matter—but it shows that owning print equipment is unnecessary for an MVP.

## Better business model

### Entry
£3.99–£6.99 physical card or £1.99–£4.99 e-card.

### Artifact upsell
- £9 crossword
- £12 roast newspaper
- £19 mini comic
- £19 personalized song
- £29 memory magazine

### Physical upsell
- £19 poster
- £29 booklet
- £49 hardcover
- £69 premium gift box

The target AOV should be closer to **£15–£30**, not £3.

## Product concepts

### 1. Crossword Card
Front: “Your birthday present is a puzzle about your own life.”
QR → 15-clue crossword.

### 2. Roast Card
Front: custom headline.
QR → full fake newspaper.

### 3. Song Card
Front: lyric/title art.
QR → private player + lyrics.

### 4. Comic Card
Front: first comic panel.
QR → 12–20 page story.

### 5. Family Memory Card
QR → relatives' audio clips, stories and photos.

### 6. Pet Card
Pet portrait on card.
QR → pet story / animation / coloring book.

## The retention moat

Moonpig is strong because occasions recur. Copy the *mechanism*, not the catalogue:

- save recipient
- birthday/anniversary
- relationship
- interests
- prior gifts
- prior photos/stories
- next year: “Make Sarah’s 2027 gift from last year’s memories + new ones?”

Over time, the buyer’s private relationship graph becomes the switching cost.

## Best launch positioning

Do **not** launch as “Moonpig but AI.”

Launch one extremely legible artifact:
> **The card that contains a crossword about you.**

Then:
> **The card that contains your relationship comic.**

Then unify under a broader brand after one works.

## Strategic score

- Generic AI card maker: **4/10**
- Cheapest cards: **2/10**
- AI message writer: **1/10**
- Artifact-card wedge: **9/10**
- Recipient-memory graph + recurring gifting: **9.5/10 long-term**, but only after a single artifact converts.
