# Specialization Playbooks

## 1. Voice Commerce Concierge — first build

### Best customers
- high-ticket Shopify stores where pre-sale questions matter: water systems, espresso equipment, ergonomic furniture, outdoor living, workshop equipment, premium pet products, specialty fitness;
- stores where humans already take sales/support calls or display “call us” prominently;
- AOV preferably >$250 and especially >$500.

### What it resolves
- product questions grounded in catalog truth;
- order status;
- eligibility questions;
- capture a callback with full context;
- send product/cart/checkout link by SMS;
- route high-intent buyers to a human;
- after-hours coverage.

### Avoid
- autonomous medical/safety advice;
- changing shipping addresses unless merchant explicitly enables it;
- outbound marketing without consent;
- refunds/financial mutations without explicit policy and verification.

### Offer
“Your store gets a 24/7 phone product specialist. It resolves the boring calls, sends links by text, and hands your team only the buyers/issues worth human time.”

### Moat
- SKU/product FAQ graph;
- call intent and conversion outcomes;
- vertical-specific troubleshooting trees;
- merchant escalation policy;
- human close feedback.

## 2. Warranty & Parts Claims Intelligence

### Best customers
Home/utility products, furniture, equipment, electronics accessories, coffee gear, pet hardware, workshop products — anything with parts and warranties.

### Core workflow
order verification → warranty check → prior claims → evidence request → defect/part classification → approved remedy under threshold OR human review → parts/replacement history → supplier/SKU defect analytics.

### Killer differentiator
Do not stop at “claims portal.” Aggregate:
- repeated failure mode by SKU/batch/supplier;
- replacement-part frequency;
- cost per claim;
- first-contact resolution;
- fraudulent/repeated claims;
- reasons a human overrode the agent.

That data can inform sourcing and product-page changes.

## 3. B2B Reorder Agent

### Best customers
Toner, packaging, wholesale coffee, cleaning supplies, consumables, replacement parts, office supplies, hospitality supplies, pet/trade supplies.

### Core workflow
predict due account → account-specific catalog/pricing → inbound or consented outbound voice/SMS → reconstruct usual basket → handle quantity changes → create draft order/invoice → rep escalation for exceptions.

### Moat
reorder cadence + product substitution + company pricing + rep notes + actual close history.

### Distribution
Shopify B2B agencies; distributors migrating phone/email ordering online; Shopify stores already using reorder apps.

## 4. Delivery Exception Resolution

### Core workflow
AfterShip/carrier webhook → severity → order value/VIP/history → recommended intervention → proactive SMS/email/voice or team alert → resolution outcome.

### Differentiate from tracking apps
Tracking pages answer “where is it?” after the customer asks. This product asks **“which shipment is about to create a refund, chargeback, or angry customer, and what is the cheapest safe intervention?”**

### Moat
carrier × lane × SKU × warehouse exception outcomes; cost of intervention vs refund/dispute avoided.

## 5. Return Rescue Agent

### Best customers
High-AOV categories where many “returns” are actually setup/compatibility/expectation problems, not fashion fit.

### Core workflow
return intent → reason → damage goes to claims → troubleshooting where appropriate → exchange → store credit → standard return/human.

### Critical metric
**measured retained contribution margin**, not tickets deflected.

### Distribution
Can sit *in front of* Loop/AfterShip/ReturnGO rather than replacing them. This avoids rebuilding carrier/label infrastructure and positions the product as a revenue layer.
