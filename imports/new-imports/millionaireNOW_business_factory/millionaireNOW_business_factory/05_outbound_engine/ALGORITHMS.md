# Algorithms — Outbound Engine

## Commercial unit
Do not sell “AI outbound”. Sell a held qualified meeting in one narrowly defined vertical.

Track this chain explicitly:
`prospects researched -> deliverable contacts -> positive replies -> qualified -> booked -> held -> sale -> gross profit`.

The only useful CAC is ultimately cost per held qualified meeting / acquired customer.

## Qualification
The starter uses transparent public-site evidence. Upgrade it with a vertical-specific checklist, e.g. MSP: staff count band, multiple locations, M365 indicators, open IT roles, compliance burden. Never invent a pain just to personalize a message.

## Drafting
The system produces one-to-one drafts from evidence and requires human approval. This is intentional: indiscriminate automated cold email creates deliverability, legal and brand risk.

## Learning loop
For each niche, fit reply and meeting likelihood from observed outcomes. Features should be business attributes and real evidence, not sensitive personal data. Use the model to prioritize research, not to spam more people.
