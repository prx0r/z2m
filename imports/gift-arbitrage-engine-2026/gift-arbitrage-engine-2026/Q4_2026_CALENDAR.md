# Q4 2026 Execution Calendar

Snapshot date: 4 September 2026.

## 4–14 September — choose wedges

- Build samples for 5 products max.
- Obtain real physical samples from at least two POD suppliers.
- Publish initial Etsy listings with honest AI/production-partner disclosure.
- Build a Shopify waitlist/checkout for products needing richer personalization.
- Record raw-input → finished-artifact transformation videos.

Recommended first five tests:

1. Family Annual / Christmas Newspaper
2. AI Family Game Night Deck
3. 2027 Year-Ahead Astrology Hardcover
4. Advent Calendar Refill
5. Memory Card / Mini-Book Card

## 15 September–5 October — collect real intent

- Test 3 creative styles per product, not 20 products.
- Track: listing views, favorites, add-to-cart, checkout, input-form completion, manual minutes/order, refund/support rate.
- Improve the personalization form before automating more generation.
- Start Pinterest content now; holiday planning begins well before December.

## 6–20 October — pick winners

- Kill low-intent listings.
- Move top 1–2 concepts to better packaging and real product photography.
- Negotiate/validate local print routes in UK/US/EU.
- Build digital fallback for every physical SKU.
- Capture buyer recipient/occasion data only with clear consent.

## 21 October–10 November — products that must arrive early

Push:
- Advent refills;
- family calendars;
- custom books;
- overseas gifts;
- ornaments;
- party/table packs.

Publish shipping cutoffs visibly and conservatively.

## 11–30 November — BFCM + gift-intent peak

The offer should be about **certainty and completed creative work**, not merely a 40% discount.

Strong hooks:
- “Upload photos; your gift is designed in 2 minutes.”
- “The family gift nobody else can buy.”
- “We pick the photos for you.”
- “A whole personalized Christmas table from one guest list.”
- “Your 2027 year-ahead book, made for one person.”

## 1–12 December — core physical personalized window

- Favor local POD.
- Add expedited/tracked options.
- Reduce customization choices where they slow production.
- Show exact “order by” deadlines.

## 13–19 December — fast local + semi-digital

Promote:
- locally printed cards/newspapers;
- print-at-home games;
- digital astrology/numerology with “hardcover gift voucher arriving later”;
- QR memory pages;
- letter PDFs.

## 20–24 December — digital rescue products

Physical ads should stop when delivery can no longer be promised.

Sell:
- instant personalized crossword;
- scavenger-hunt / gift-reveal pack;
- downloadable family newspaper;
- digital year-ahead report;
- printable “physical book is being made” certificate;
- video/QR memory experience.

## 26 December–10 January — Q5 / New Year

This is an unusually good window for:

- 2027 astrology year-ahead;
- 2027 numerology/personal-year products;
- personalized annual journal;
- family calendar;
- “our year in review” products;
- thank-you cards;
- annual memory subscriptions.

## The anti-seasonality strategy

Each Q4 winner should have an evergreen twin:

- Christmas family newspaper → birthday/anniversary/retirement newspaper;
- 2027 astrology → birthday solar-return / natal book;
- cracker trivia → wedding/table trivia;
- Christmas photo book → Mother’s/Father’s Day;
- advent refill → 30-day couple/kids challenge;
- family game → reunion/wedding/holiday-rental game.
