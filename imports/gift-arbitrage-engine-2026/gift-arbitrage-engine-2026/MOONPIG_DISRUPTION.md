# Moonpig Disruption Strategy: Build the Occasion OS, Not a Cheaper Card

## The mistake

Moonpig’s standard UK card is £3.99. It is tempting to argue AI makes card creation cheap enough to undercut it.

That is not the best attack. Printing was never the real moat.

Moonpig’s FY26 public metrics include 12.3M active customers, 36.0M Moonpig/Greetz orders, £9.32 AOV, 113M saved occasion reminders and a 17.9% gift attach rate. Cards are a recurring event-triggered acquisition and retention loop.

## The wedge: “I made this in 30 seconds, but it looks like I spent an evening”

### Product 1 — Memory Card

Input:
- recipient;
- 6–30 photos;
- occasion;
- optional one-line note.

System:
- dedupe / quality rank;
- select 4–10 strongest memories;
- cluster by chronology or theme;
- draft concise captions only from user-supplied context;
- make three layouts;
- show instant preview.

Output:
- 8-page mini-book/card rather than a single folded card.

Suggested test price: roughly **£7.99–£12.99**, not £2.99. The point is to increase perceived value and eliminate work, not wage a paper-price war.

### Product 2 — Gift attach

Once a buyer has supplied recipient context, offer exactly 2–3 attachments:

- QR memory ornament;
- 52-memory card deck;
- 20-page mini photo book;
- handwritten recipe keepsake when relevant.

Do not show a giant generic catalog. Use recipient/occasion data to make the choice easier.

## The compounding data model

With consent, save:

- recipient name / relation;
- important dates;
- postal address;
- gift history;
- style/tone preferences;
- approved photo collection references;
- last year’s artifact;
- reminders.

Next year the CTA becomes:

> “Mum’s birthday is in 12 days. We drafted this year’s card from the photos you uploaded. Review it?”

That is much more defensible than “AI can write card copy.”

## Acquisition ladder

1. **Free/cheap digital:** instant personalized crossword, card or “gift reveal” PDF.
2. **£8–£15:** Memory Card / mini-book card.
3. **£20–£50:** ornament, deck, newspaper, calendar.
4. **£50–£150:** hardcover books / group tribute / astrology annual.
5. **Annual recurrence:** Christmas annual, calendar, astrology year ahead, advent refill.

## One-click photo book angle

Chatbooks already validates automatic photo selection with Smart Select. Its current help page says the feature does not yet select a specific person. That is a concrete wedge for gifting: “build a book about Dad/Mum/us/the dog.”

Source: https://help.chatbooks.com/en/articles/16531937-what-is-smart-select

## MVP architecture

Do not start with Google Photos OAuth and a 100-SKU catalog.

V0:
- upload 10–60 images;
- choose recipient/occasion;
- AI selects + lays out;
- user approves;
- generate a print-ready PDF;
- send to Prodigi/Gelato/Lulu depending format;
- capture recipient occasion for reminder.

Only add photo-library integrations after measuring whether selection automation materially raises conversion.

## What would make this win

- creation time under 2 minutes;
- preview before payment or immediately after minimal input;
- reliable local delivery and clear cutoffs;
- outputs that look materially more “worked on” than a standard card;
- no hallucinated memories;
- exceptional mobile UX;
- reminders and pre-drafted future gifts;
- 3–5 hero products, not a 5,000-SKU catalog.
