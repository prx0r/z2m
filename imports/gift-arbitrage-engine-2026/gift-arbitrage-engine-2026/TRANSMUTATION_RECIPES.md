# Transmutation Recipes — Reusable Personalized-Gift Formulas

The engine is more valuable than any single SKU. These formulas can be moved between Etsy, Shopify, Amazon Custom, TikTok content, corporate gifting and local POD.

## Recipe 1 — Memory Compression

**Raw input:** too many photos/messages/memories.

**AI job:** select, dedupe, rank, organize, caption.

**Artifact:** book, newspaper, calendar, card set, ornament archive.

**Why it works:** customer’s real cost is *selection labor*.

Examples:
- one-click Mum photo book;
- Family Times 2026;
- pet yearbook;
- 12 Months of Us cards.

## Recipe 2 — Group Contribution → One Coherent Artifact

**Raw input:** 5–100 people each contribute a message/photo.

**AI job:** chase structure, normalize tone/length, remove duplicates, arrange into narrative.

**Artifact:** retirement book, birthday tribute, client annual, wedding newspaper.

**Moat:** every contributor is a distribution edge.

## Recipe 3 — Profile → Premium Report

**Raw input:** structured attributes/date/time/place/preferences.

**Deterministic layer:** calculate factual/technical structure first.

**AI job:** interpret, explain, personalize tone, build journal prompts.

**Artifact:** astrology year-ahead, numerology annual, personalized reflection journal.

**Rule:** never ask the model to invent calculated facts.

## Recipe 4 — Personal Facts → Game

**Raw input:** names, facts, locations, inside jokes, photos.

**AI job:** generate clues/questions, balance difficulty, remove awkward content.

**Artifact:** family trivia deck, crossword, scavenger hunt, mystery, playing cards.

**Why it works:** writing a bespoke game is high apparent labor and near-zero AI marginal cost.

## Recipe 5 — Source Material → Heirloom

**Raw input:** handwriting, recipe, old photo, voice recording, child drawing.

**AI job:** restore/transcribe/clean while preserving the original identity.

**Artifact:** towel, book, print, ornament, QR keepsake.

**Critical rule:** preservation beats “improvement.” Always let the buyer approve transcription/restoration.

## Recipe 6 — Date + Place + Relationship → Meaningful Visual

**Raw input:** date/place/names/memory.

**Deterministic layer:** real map/sky/weather/calendar data.

**AI job:** narrative and design assembly.

**Artifact:** “night we met” data story, first-home piece, wedding day print.

**Lesson from star maps:** the formula is proven; plain star maps are saturated. Combine multiple verified signals and a personal story.

## Recipe 7 — Occasion Guest List → Variable-Data Event Pack

**Raw input:** people attending the same event.

**AI job:** make each person’s component unique while keeping a coherent theme.

**Artifact:** Christmas crackers, place cards, awards, trivia, menu cards, wedding newspaper.

**Why it works:** traditional design labor scales linearly with guest count; AI does not.

## Recipe 8 — Voice → Persistent Physical Token

**Raw input:** authorized voice note.

**AI job:** trim/noise-clean/transcribe, create visual, host private playback.

**Artifact:** QR card, ornament, acrylic block, family archive.

**Moat:** hosted memory page can become a recurring family archive.

## Recipe 9 — One Upload → Product Fan-Out

**Raw input:** one valuable family source asset (recipe, photo set, voice, house image).

**AI job:** create consistent design system.

**Outputs:** towel + card + print + book + deck + ornament.

**Economics:** acquisition and personalization cost is paid once; bundle AOV rises.

## Recipe 10 — Annual State → Recurring Artifact

**Raw input:** photos/events/preferences accumulated over a year.

**AI job:** summarize/curate.

**Artifact:** annual newspaper, calendar, year-ahead report, advent refill, pet annual.

**Moat:** every year’s data makes the next year easier to sell.

## Recipe 11 — High-Value Relationship → Trivial Gift Budget

**Context:** the recipient relationship is economically valuable.

**Examples:** client, employee retirement, realtor closing, wedding party, key customer.

**Artifact:** $100–$500 personalized book/box/magazine.

**Why it works:** price is anchored to relationship value, not COGS.

## Recipe 12 — Digital Urgency Escape Hatch

Every physical Q4 product should have a “too late to ship” version:

- instant PDF;
- printable gift certificate showing the physical item is coming;
- interactive web edition;
- downloadable puzzle/letter/card.

This lets the business keep selling on Dec 20–24 after physical cutoffs.

## A reusable opportunity equation

Score a new idea on:

`proven demand × personalization desire × AI labor removed × emotional stakes × visible transformation × Q4/occasion multiplier × fulfillment simplicity × recurrence`

Then subtract:

`saturation + IP risk + privacy sensitivity + quality failure risk + manual support burden`.

The included engine implements this logic in `src/giftradar/scoring.py`.
