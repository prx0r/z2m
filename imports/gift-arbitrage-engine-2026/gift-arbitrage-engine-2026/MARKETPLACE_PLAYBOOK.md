# Marketplace Playbook

## Etsy — prove search demand and collect reviews

Use Etsy for products the buyer can understand from a single listing:

- family newspaper;
- custom game deck;
- recipe heirloom;
- QR ornament;
- astrology hardcover;
- advent refill;
- crossword;
- pet yearbook.

### Etsy implementation rules

Etsy’s Creativity Standards currently allow seller-prompted AI creations with disclosure and seller-designed items produced by disclosed production partners. Buyer-personalized items can also be produced by a production partner. The current Seller Policy requires accurate information about production, shipping origin and AI usage.

Sources:
- https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity/
- https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/

Do not sell generic mass-resold goods disguised as handmade. Do not sell AI prompt bundles.

Printify’s current Etsy personalization integration can map buyer-entered text/options/images directly into design layers and supports live preview. Etsy personalization supports a maximum of five personalization questions in the current Printify workflow, so complex products should push the buyer to a post-purchase/private form or owned-site funnel only where marketplace rules allow it.

Source: https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/40720228428433-How-do-I-set-up-automated-personalization-for-Etsy

### Listing strategy

Show the transformation, not a generic mockup:

1. raw inputs;
2. “AI organizes it” intermediate frame;
3. finished artifact;
4. detail spread;
5. personalization flow;
6. delivery/preview promise.

## Shopify — build the actual moat

Owned site is best for:

- batch photo upload;
- group contribution links;
- photo/recipient graph;
- account reminders;
- annual subscription/refills;
- premium upsells;
- private audio pages;
- complex multi-step previews.

Use marketplace listings as discovery, but the long-term company is an occasion/recipient system.

## Amazon Custom / Handmade

Best for simple, proven SKUs with clear text/photo personalization and high search intent. Avoid using Amazon as the first surface for complex “upload 100 photos and collaborate with 8 family members” workflows.

## TikTok Shop / short-form video

Use products where the transformation is satisfying in 10–30 seconds:

- pet photo → newspaper;
- 20 photos → family annual;
- family facts → game deck;
- handwriting → heirloom bundle;
- guest list → personalized Christmas crackers.

Printify currently documents automated personalization on TikTok as text-only, so image-heavy personalized products may need a different order flow.

Source: https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/28903711308177-What-is-product-personalization-on-Printify

## Pinterest

Excellent for products with strong visual planning behavior: stationery, correspondence, hosting, weddings, nostalgic books, ornaments and holiday tables. Pinterest Predicts 2026’s “Pen Pals” signal makes letter/card products especially timely.

Source: https://business.pinterest.com/en-gb/pdf/pinterest-predicts/2026-trend-report/

## UK premium gifting marketplaces

Use curated UK channels as a second-stage distribution route once there are real product photos, reliable fulfillment and reviews. The positioning should be premium/meaningful, not “AI-generated.” AI is the production advantage; the customer buys the finished emotional artifact.

## Cross-marketplace recipe

For every validated concept create:

- **Etsy:** exact-intent SKU/listing;
- **Shopify:** easiest personalized workflow + bundles + reminders;
- **Pinterest:** visual inspiration pins;
- **TikTok/Meta:** transformation video;
- **Google Search/Shopping where applicable:** intent capture (“personalized family newspaper”, “custom astrology book”);
- **B2B outbound:** high-value version of same engine.
