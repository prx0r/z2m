# 30-Day Execution Plan: 4 September → 4 October 2026

## Goal

Do not build a huge ecommerce platform. Build a **trend evidence engine + 3 sellable tests**.

## Days 1–2 — data foundation

- Open/free Pinterest Business account if not already available.
- Claim the target domain(s).
- Export all available Analytics CSVs.
- Run `code/pinterest_export_analyzer.py`.
- Create a simple SQLite/CSV archive for trend snapshots.
- Open Pinterest Trends on desktop and capture 2-year curves for 30 seed keywords.

Seed families:

- evening dress / party dress / burgundy dress / velvet dress;
- casual blazer outfit;
- gothic nails / Halloween nails / lace nails;
- birthday magazine / personalized magazine / photo zine;
- tracking journal / photo album / memory box;
- clay bag charm / beaded bag charm / embroidery kit;
- coffee station / coffee gift / coffee bar sign;
- equestrian aesthetic / barn jacket / rosette;
- trinket shelf / display shelf;
- scent layering / fragrance organizer.

## Days 3–4 — Etsy validation

Use the 15 free Marketplace Insights searches strategically. Do not waste them on broad words.

Priority:

1. birthday magazine
2. personalized magazine
3. photo zine
4. Halloween press on nails
5. gothic press on nails
6. bag charm kit
7. embroidery kit beginner
8. coffee bar sign
9. coffee recipe cards
10. personalized journal
11. memory book
12. horse girl gift
13. brooch gift
14. velvet bag
15. scent journal

Record search count, listings and related terms.

## Days 5–6 — ad/commerce validation

For top 10 concepts:

- search Meta Ad Library in UK + US;
- log first-seen/visible longevity and creative count;
- use TikTok Creative Center country/category filters;
- inspect TikTok Shop via free/public views where possible;
- use Google Trends for 5-year recurrence and country differences;
- read 10–30 Reddit threads/comments for objection language.

## Day 7 — first scoring gate

Score all ideas with the 100-point model.

Kill anything under 70 unless it has a specific unmodeled advantage.

Select 3:

1. personalized memory magazine;
2. seasonal nail/aesthetic accessory family;
3. beginner craft kit or home-café personalized gift, depending supplier economics.

## Days 8–10 — offers, not stores

Build a one-page offer for each.

Required:

- specific buyer/recipient;
- specific occasion;
- 3–5 variants;
- clear preview;
- truthful delivery promise;
- returns/reprint rules;
- analytics events;
- one checkout path.

## Days 11–13 — creative batch

Create 12–20 Pins per concept using distinct archetypes:

- clean product;
- collage;
- “gift for X”;
- before/after personalization;
- tutorial/demo;
- aesthetic moodboard;
- result reveal;
- recipient reaction concept;
- under-£X gift guide;
- seasonal countdown.

Do not use fake reviews, fake scarcity or misleading AI imagery of products that cannot actually be fulfilled.

## Days 14–17 — organic Pinterest test

Publish consistently and measure:

- impressions/day;
- saves/1k;
- outbound/1k;
- pin clicks/1k;
- landing-page CTR;
- add-to-cart and purchase.

Require at least two creative archetypes to show life before declaring a concept robust.

## Days 18–21 — cheap paid confirmation

Only on concepts with organic/outbound evidence.

Use small spend to compare creative/offer fit; do not ask paid ads to rescue a weak landing page.

## Days 22–24 — supplier / fulfillment hardening

Verify:

- sample quality;
- actual delivery times;
- packaging;
- gift-readiness;
- damaged/reprint procedure;
- Christmas cutoff;
- tracking;
- margin after all fees.

## Days 25–27 — localization scan

Run UK winner keywords through Norway/Denmark/Sweden/other affluent candidates.

Look for:

- demand without equivalent advertiser density;
- easy local-language keyword adaptation;
- acceptable shipping/returns;
- local currency/payment trust.

## Days 28–30 — scale one, archive everything

Scale only the concept with:

- persistent Pin performance;
- outbound intent;
- at least one transaction or strong checkout evidence;
- defensible fulfillment;
- 10+ creative variants still available.

Archive every failed concept with evidence so the engine does not retest dead ideas blindly.

## Q4 operating cadence after day 30

Daily:

- 15-minute trend snapshot;
- current ads/competitor changes;
- own Pin outliers.

Weekly:

- score refresh;
- new Etsy Marketplace Insights searches;
- 10 new product concepts;
- one local-market scan;
- winner/loser postmortem.

Monthly:

- save raw trends snapshots;
- compare forecast versus actual;
- update scoring weights based on realized purchases, not impressions.
