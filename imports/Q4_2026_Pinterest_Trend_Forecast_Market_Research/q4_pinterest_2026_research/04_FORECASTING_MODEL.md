# Forecasting Model and Scorecard

## Why a scorecard matters

The common failure mode in trend research is narrative seduction: one huge growth number makes an idea feel inevitable. A scorecard forces independent evidence.

## Q4 Product Opportunity Score (0–100)

### A. Pinterest persistence — 20 points

- 0: single spike / one Pin
- 5: 2–3 weeks or one creative
- 10: ≥2 months or several Pins
- 15: several months + several creatives
- 20: persistent, multi-month, multi-creative, increasing outbound intent

### B. Current Pinterest acceleration — 15 points

Use current spotlight, Trends UI, API growth and your own pin velocity.

### C. Seasonal recurrence — 15 points

Use Q4 2024/Q4 2025 curves where available. Reward stable recurrence and useful lead time.

### D. Commerce intent — 10 points

Outbound clicks, Etsy search behavior, marketplace sales/GMV, purchase-intent language.

### E. Cross-platform confirmation — 10 points

Google, TikTok, Meta, Etsy, Reddit. Full points require multiple *different kinds* of evidence, not five scraper sites repeating one source.

### F. Economics — 10 points

Contribution margin, shipping, returns, replacement risk, payment/marketplace fees.

### G. Creative surface area — 5 points

Can the product support 10–30 legitimate creative angles?

### H. Giftability/personalization — 5 points

Q4 bonus: recipient/occasion value, easy wrapping, perceived thoughtfulness, personalization.

### I. Execution speed — 5 points

Can it be launched within days rather than months?

### J. Localization portability — 5 points

Can the same offer be deployed into UK/NO/DK/SE/etc with copy/currency/fulfillment changes rather than a new product?

## Penalties

Subtract up to 30 points for:

- IP/counterfeit dependency: -5 to -20
- hazardous/regulatory complexity: -5 to -15
- fragile/bulky shipping: -3 to -10
- sizing/returns: -3 to -8
- single-supplier fragility: -3 to -8
- no believable differentiation: -5 to -15
- already-late seasonal timing: -5 to -20

## Forecast confidence

Do not confuse opportunity score with probability of success.

Label confidence:

- **High:** first-party trend history + live acceleration + commerce evidence + economics verified.
- **Medium:** trend and cross-platform evidence strong, but sales/economics not fully verified.
- **Low:** based mainly on social momentum or a single vendor estimate.

## Trend-stage classification

### Stage 0 — weak signal

One source, no recurrence.

### Stage 1 — emerging

2+ independent sources, low competition, growth visible.

### Stage 2 — validated

Persistent first-party search/save behavior + commerce proof.

### Stage 3 — scaling

Multiple advertisers/sellers/creatives with sustained spend/sales.

### Stage 4 — saturated

High seller density, falling growth, poor differentiation economics.

The best low-capital entries are usually late Stage 1 or early Stage 2.

## A better “800k” equivalent

If analyzing your own account, calculate percentile thresholds:

- P90 impressions/day;
- P75 saves/1k;
- P75 outbound clicks/1k.

A “serious product” should ideally have at least three Pins meeting two of those thresholds across multiple weeks, rather than simply one Pin passing an absolute 800k count.

## Cross-country arbitrage score

For each validated concept and country:

`geo_score = 0.30*demand + 0.20*competition_inverse + 0.15*affluence + 0.15*shipping_feasibility + 0.10*localization_ease + 0.10*ad_cost_advantage`

All inputs normalized 0–1.

This is consistent with the prior project preference: port proven playbooks into affluent markets where local competition is lower, rather than assuming the UK is always the final market.
