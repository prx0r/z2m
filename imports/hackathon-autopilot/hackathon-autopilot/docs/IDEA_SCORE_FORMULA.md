# Idea Score Formula

Default score out of 100:

- 20 rubric fit
- 15 sponsor centrality
- 15 demo clarity
- 10 originality
- 10 technical depth
- 10 real-world/startup viability
- 10 evidence/provenance potential
- 5 reuse leverage
- 5 safety/control clarity

Subtract:
- 0–20 integration risk
- 0–15 build complexity
- 0–10 dependency/credential risk
- 0–10 demo fragility

An idea scoring 80 with a clean 3-minute transformation should usually beat a 92-point concept that requires six unproven services.
