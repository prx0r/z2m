# Winner Storytelling Patterns

Winning repos and demos repeatedly compress complexity into a fast story.

## Pattern A — one operational incident
Used by projects such as emergency-response and first-responder winners.

Structure:
1. concrete person/event
2. costly failure in current workflow
3. live intervention
4. visible outcome
5. human remains at the meaningful boundary

## Pattern B — demo above the fold
Winning READMEs frequently make the fastest path:
- watch demo
- open live app
- understand TL;DR
before deep technical sections.

## Pattern C — truthfully distinguish prototype vs later wrapper
A strong repo says what ran during the hackathon and what is a later presentation layer.

## Pattern D — one sentence with a sharp inversion
Examples of form:
- "The best dashboard is one you don't need to look at."
- "Report once. File everywhere."
- "Voice banking without a smartphone."
- "Measure the name before you buy it."

## Pattern E — proof of depth after clarity
Architecture, datasets, tests, model cards, limitations, and technical appendices come after the judge understands the product.
