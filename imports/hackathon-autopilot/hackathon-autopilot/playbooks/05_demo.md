# Playbook 05 — Canonical Demo

## Demo structure

### 0:00–0:20 — Hook
One problem. One surprising thesis.

### 0:20–0:40 — Mechanism
Show the pipeline. Name the sponsor. Explain why sponsor data/action is indispensable.

### 0:40–2:00 — Live transformation
Run one scenario end to end.

### 2:00–2:25 — Proof
Receipt, provenance, audit, readback, deterministic check, or before/after result.

### 2:25–2:45 — Product / moat
Explain what reusable product exists beyond the demo.

### 2:45–3:00 — Close
Repeat the memorable thesis in a sharper form.

Adjust to required video length.

## Demo rule
Do not narrate six research findings while the real product is idle. Let the product do work on screen.

## Best ending
End on the consequence/proof, not a generic homepage.
