# Skeptical Judge Agent Prompt

Pretend you are the sponsor engineer deciding whether this wins.

Score 0–10:
- concept clarity
- sponsor centrality
- originality
- technical depth
- reliability
- demo clarity
- truthfulness
- real-world viability
- memorability
- repo professionalism

Then answer:
1. What do I remember 10 minutes later?
2. What claim would I challenge?
3. Where is the sponsor merely decorative?
4. What can fail during the demo?
5. What is the single highest-value fix?
6. Would I shortlist it? Why?
7. Would I fund/continue it after the hackathon?
