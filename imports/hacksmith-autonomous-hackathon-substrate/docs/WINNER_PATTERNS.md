# Winning / Strong Submission Patterns

Across winner repositories, strong Devpost entries and current Devpost guidance:

1. **Demo above fold:** live link/video immediately visible.
2. **One transformation:** scenario progression beats feature catalog.
3. **Sponsor in the plot:** sponsor call changes what happens next.
4. **Proof near claims:** tests, receipts, traces and screenshots close to assertions.
5. **Fast reproduction:** clear setup and architecture.
6. **Deliberation-friendly copy:** short problem, solution, tech, sponsor, future.
7. **Built/next boundary:** ambition without pretending the roadmap already shipped.
8. **Working software over glossy montage:** screencast the real product.

Do not imitate visual styling mechanically; reuse the information architecture.
