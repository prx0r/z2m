# External Sources

Always prioritize the specific event's official rules and sponsor docs. These sources inform generic submission structure:

1. Devpost Help Center — Video-making best practices (updated May 27, 2026)  
https://help.devpost.com/article/84-video-making-best-practices  
Screencast functioning product; elevator pitch in opening seconds; script/rehearse; leave upload time.

2. Devpost — 6 Tips for making a winning hackathon demo video  
https://info.devpost.com/blog/6-tips-for-making-a-hackathon-demo-video  
Center the problem/story, show real input/output, address judging criteria, keep the demo simple and short.

3. Devpost Help Center — Judging & public voting  
https://help.devpost.com/article/64-judging-public-voting  
Recurring criteria include idea quality, implementation and potential impact.

4. Devpost — Understanding hackathon submission and judging criteria  
https://info.devpost.com/blog/understanding-hackathon-submission-and-judging-criteria  
Verify required tools, URLs, video limits; technological implementation and ease of use matter.

5. Devpost Help Center — Know Your Submission Steps (2026)  
https://help.devpost.com/article/126-know-your-submission-steps  
Common fields include Built With, Try It Out, image gallery and public video link.

6. Hackathon Playbook — Submission (updated June 24, 2026)  
https://thehackathonplaybook.dev/playbook/submission  
Supporting practitioner guidance: Devpost, README and video persist into deliberation; technical judges inspect clean repos/architecture.

7. Representative Devpost rule sets commonly require a public repo, functioning demo footage, setup instructions, screenshots/architecture and short video limits; exact rules vary by event.
