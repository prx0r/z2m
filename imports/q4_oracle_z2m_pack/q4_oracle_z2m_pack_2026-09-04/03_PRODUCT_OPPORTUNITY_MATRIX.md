## Required competition validation

Before an opportunity can move from `RESEARCHED` to `TEST_READY`, collect:
- exact marketplace result count or Marketplace Insights competition measure;
- top-20 median price and review count;
- number of sellers with meaningful sales/reviews;
- Google Trends 5-year shape and current 90-day slope;
- Keyword Planner volume/CPC where available;
- Google Shopping seller count / price dispersion;
- Pinterest trend/search slope if applicable;
- supplier count, lead time and landed cost;
- IP/compliance risk;
- actual contribution after fees, shipping, returns and payment processing.

Do not label an opportunity "low competition" based only on an LLM or trend report.
