# Supplier / Partner Outreach

## Retail supplier message

Subject: Online reseller / direct-dispatch enquiry

Hello,

We are launching a specialist online buying experience for [CATEGORY]. The site helps customers configure the right setup from a structured catalogue, while an established UK supplier retains inventory and fulfilment.

We are interested in opening a reseller/trade account and would like to understand:

1. Trade discount / margin by product category.
2. Whether you can dispatch individual orders directly to our end customers.
3. Whether shipments can be neutral / omit your retail invoice and marketing.
4. Stock availability feed (CSV/API or portal export).
5. Product data available: SKU, GTIN, specs, images, manuals, warranty.
6. Delivery cost and cutoff times.
7. Returns/RMA process and restocking charges.
8. Warranty/service responsibility.
9. Payment terms initially and whether credit terms are available after trading history.
10. Any brand/MAP/advertising restrictions.

We will initially validate with a small number of manually processed orders rather than requesting integration work.

Regards,
[Business]

# Commercial facts to capture in the database

Do not call something a viable dropship supplier until these are known:

- exact wholesale price
- VAT treatment
- direct-to-customer permission
- neutral packing
- shipping cost
- warranty owner
- return destination
- restocking fee
- live stock method
- payment timing
- credit terms
- brand advertising restrictions
