# Google Ads Playbook

## Universal rule

Never optimize for revenue alone.

### Ecommerce

`pre_ad_contribution = net_sale - supplier_cost - delivery - payment_fee - expected_returns_support`

`break_even_CPC = pre_ad_contribution * conversion_rate`

### Lead model

`expected_value_per_lead = close_rate * gross_fee_per_close`

`break_even_CPC = click_to_lead_rate * expected_value_per_lead`

Use conservative rates until you have real data.

## Garden rooms

**Campaign:** Search only initially. One geographic area. Exact + phrase match.

Ad groups:
1. garden room quote
2. garden office installer
3. garden room cost
4. premium/luxury garden room

Examples:
- [garden room quote kent]
- "garden office installer surrey"
- [garden room builders sussex]

Negatives:
DIY, free, plans, jobs, used, shed, cheap, pinterest, images, course.

Landing promise:
**See what a premium garden room could look like in your garden, then get a verified local survey.**

Conversion: completed qualification + phone/email + consent.

## Golf simulator

**Do not launch paid ads until contribution is known.**

Campaign after trade terms:
1. garage simulator systems
2. complete simulator packages
3. small-room compatibility
4. specific launch monitor + simulator intent

Examples:
- [golf simulator package uk]
- "golf simulator for garage"
- [home golf simulator installation]
- "skytrak simulator package"

Negatives:
free game, software crack, jobs, venue booking, nearest indoor golf, used if not supported.

Landing promise:
**Tell us your room size. We will build the compatible setup.**

## Commercial coffee equipment

Campaign: Search with quote conversion.

Ad groups:
1. cafe machine quote
2. 2-group espresso machine
3. office coffee machine
4. equipment finance / lease
5. new cafe equipment

Examples:
- [commercial coffee machine quote]
- "espresso machine for cafe"
- [2 group coffee machine finance]
- "office coffee machine lease"

Negatives:
home, pods, cheap, second hand (unless monetized), jobs, training-only, repair-only.

Landing promise:
**Tell us drinks/day, peak volume and budget. Get a correctly sized machine + grinder + water + finance shortlist.**

# Account-level testing discipline

- Separate each vertical/account where legally and operationally appropriate.
- Manual budgets initially.
- No broad match until conversion data exists.
- No Performance Max until the offer and conversion tracking are proven.
- Record contribution/commission, not just ROAS.
- Route query text back into advisor copy and landing pages.
