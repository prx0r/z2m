# Market Research — AI-Native Premium Retail / Brokerage

**Date:** 4 September 2026  
**Objective:** find the best low-capital niches where a solo technical operator owns demand + premium UX + AI qualification while suppliers/installers handle inventory or labour.

## Scoring method

100-point score:

- Existing high-intent demand — 20
- Transaction economics — 20
- AI advisor / visualization advantage — 15
- Supplier or fulfilment route — 15
- Operational simplicity — 10
- Cheap/fast ad validation — 10
- Ability to compound into data, supplier rights, recurring revenue or brand — 10

Scores are strategic estimates based on the cited evidence. They are **not profitability forecasts**. No paid campaign should launch until real supplier margin/referral economics are confirmed.

---

## 1. Garden rooms / premium garden offices — 92/100 — BUILD FIRST

### Why it wins

This is the cleanest low-capital test because you do not need inventory or trade credit. You can acquire and qualify a £25k+ homeowner project, visualize the proposed outcome, then sell/book the survey to a local installer.

A current UK operator, Garden Room Leads, explicitly markets pre-qualified homeowners with **£25k+ budgets**, says it is working with three South-East garden-room companies, and shows an **AI-generated photoreal render of the prospect's actual garden in 60 seconds**. This validates almost the exact thesis: premium visual experience + qualification + outsourced physical fulfilment.

Source: https://gardenroomleads.co.uk/

AceLeads also currently sells UK garden-room leads to manufacturers/installers, collecting use, size, budget, region and timeframe and using a pay-on-conversion model.

Source: https://aceleads.co.uk/garden-room-leads/

A January 2026 UK lead-generation analysis reported the keyword “garden room” at roughly 1k–10k monthly searches with indicative bids around £0.82–£2.39, while the referenced builder described £3k–£4k margin per room. Treat those figures as a third-party/operator estimate, not Google Keyword Planner data.

Source: https://answers.holschuh.co.uk/solved-uk-builder-needs-advice-on-gaining-leads-online

### Exact money flow

Google click → premium landing page → homeowner uploads garden photo + answers 6 questions → AI produces rough concept and budget band → user requests exact survey → installer receives qualified lead / booked appointment → you receive agreed lead, appointment, or closed-job fee.

### Why AI matters

- Photo + dimensions → visual concept.
- Qualify budget before wasting installer time.
- Explain permitted-development caveats without pretending to give legal advice.
- Route leads by postcode, budget and build type.
- Generate a clean installer brief and customer proposal.

### What not to build

Do not build a CAD engine or automated planning-permission checker first. Mock the visualization or use a simple image-generation pipeline after the lead submits. The conversion hypothesis is what matters.

### Google Ads first campaign

Start with one installer geography, not national UK.

High intent:
- `garden room quote [county]`
- `garden office installation [city]`
- `garden room builders [city]`
- `garden room cost [city]`
- `luxury garden office [county]`

Negatives: DIY, plans, free, jobs, used, shed-only, ideas, Pinterest.

Landing angle: **“See a garden room in your own garden before booking a survey.”**

### 48-hour test

1. Recruit 2 installers willing to accept test leads on success fee / free pilot.
2. Put up one geo-specific page.
3. Spend £30–£60 on exact/phrase long-tail search.
4. Target one real booked-survey conversion.

### Domain/brand direction (availability NOT checked)

- GardenBrief
- RoomInGarden
- StudioGarden

### Moat path

Lead-gen → booked-survey network → installer performance data → exclusive postcode territories → instant quote estimates → financing referrals → own branded modular systems.

## 2. Golf simulators / launch-monitor systems — 88/100 — BUILD PROTOTYPE, SECURE TERMS BEFORE ADS

### Why it scores highly

The UK golf simulator market is growing quickly. Straits Research estimates the UK market at **$137m in 2026**, forecasting $452m by 2034 (16.1% CAGR). Grand View also forecasts strong UK growth, though with a lower 9.3% CAGR estimate. Market-report estimates should be treated directionally rather than as audited sales data.

Sources:
- https://straitsresearch.com/report/golf-simulators-market/united-kingdom
- https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/golf-simulator-market/uk

The Financial Times reported a 2026 boom in home simulators, noting installs can range from below $1,000 to above $70,000 and citing growth in simulated golf participation.

Source: https://www.ft.com/content/f458d7d5-71bb-4f3e-b3b6-7cb7cbfdd772

GolfBays operates a UK trade account for simulator installers/suppliers with trade pricing, priority stock, fast dispatch and custom-project support. It says applications are reviewed in 1–2 business days.

Source: https://golfbays.co.uk/pages/open-a-trade-account

GolfBays also has a public affiliate program paying **5%** on qualified sales and says its average order value is **$700+**. This gives a near-zero-capital validation route before securing reseller economics.

Source: https://golfbays.co.uk/pages/affiliate-program

### Critical economics warning

A public golf keyword dataset currently lists global `golf simulator` CPC around **$4.28**. This is not UK Google Keyword Planner data and should only be used as a warning signal.

Source: https://www.theseolabs.com/keywords-lists/golf/

At a 5% affiliate commission, paid search is unlikely to be attractive unless conversion/AOV are unusually strong. **Do not buy Google Ads on affiliate economics.** Use affiliate links for organic UX validation, then secure real trade margin or a project/installation fee before paid acquisition.

### AI-native selling experience

Customer enters:
- room width/depth/height
- handedness
- budget
- existing PC/projector
- practice vs entertainment
- permanent vs retractable

Advisor returns a compatible system rather than 100 products.

The strongest future feature is photo/dimension → room visualization → exact bundle.

### Google Ads strategy after trade economics are known

- `golf simulator package UK`
- `golf simulator for garage`
- `home golf simulator installation`
- exact launch monitor + room terms

Negatives: free software, games, jobs, commercial venue booking, used (unless you sell used).

### 48-hour validation

1. Apply to GolfBays trade + affiliate programs.
2. Use affiliate route immediately for a live prototype without order fulfilment.
3. Publish 5 high-intent long-tail pages.
4. Measure advisor starts → recommendation → outbound affiliate clicks.
5. Only run paid ads once gross £ contribution per order is known.

### Domain/brand direction

- SimStudio
- HomeFairway
- GolfRoom

### Moat path

Affiliate validation → trade reseller → bundle economics → installation partners → room scan/render data → exclusive package SKUs → own enclosure/mat private label.

## 3. Commercial coffee equipment — 87/100 — BUILD PROTOTYPE

### Why this beats ordinary home espresso for low-capital EV

Commercial coffee equipment has much larger transactions and a buyer who urgently needs the equipment to run a business. A July 2026 UK guide puts new commercial espresso machines around **£3,500–£25,000**, with 2-group machines commonly £7,000–£15,000. Those are guide estimates, not wholesale margin data.

Source: https://plcoffeeshopconsulting.co.uk/insights/commercial-coffee-machine-guide-uk

The Coffee Lobby has a current authorised **reseller/distributor portal** with wholesale pricing across commercial espresso machines, grinders and brewing systems, direct manufacturer relationships, after-sales support and equipment leasing.

Source: https://www.coffeemachinestore.co.uk/

ADS Coffee Supplies says it stocks **900+ products**, supplies 10,000+ customers, offers trade pricing, next-day delivery, leasing and nationwide service/repair support.

Source: https://ads-coffee-supplies.co.uk/trade-account/

Earl Coffee explicitly offers wholesale accounts for businesses reselling through physical or online stores.

Source: https://earlcoffee.co.uk/pages/trade-wholesale-accounts

### Exact model

Do not start as “another coffee shop.” Start as **AI equipment procurement for cafés/offices/hospitality**.

Lead says:
> 80-seat café, 250 drinks/day, 2 baristas, £12k equipment budget, needs finance.

Advisor generates a shortlist/BOM and requests quote. You source via authorised reseller route; supplier/engineers handle equipment/support; leasing can reduce customer cash friction.

### AI edge

- Drinks/day and peak-hour sizing.
- Machine + grinder + water treatment compatibility.
- Power/plumbing checklist.
- Leasing-vs-buy comparison.
- Automatically produce a professional quote and supplier brief.

### Google strategy

- `commercial coffee machine quote`
- `espresso machine for cafe UK`
- `2 group coffee machine finance`
- `office coffee machine lease`
- brand + `commercial` + `quote`

Negatives: home, pod, cheap, repair-only (unless monetized), manual, jobs.

Landing angle: **“Tell us your daily volume. Get the right machine, grinder, water treatment and finance option.”**

### 48-hour test

1. Apply for reseller/wholesale access with two suppliers.
2. Build one “equipment configurator” page.
3. Cold outreach 30 newly opened / opening cafés plus £30 search test.
4. Goal: 3 quote requests, not checkout revenue.

### Domain/brand direction

- CafeEquip
- BaristaSystems
- CoffeeFitout

### Moat path

Broker/reseller → supplier terms → recurring beans/filters/service → lease/referral revenue → multi-site customer accounts → proprietary equipment-performance data.

- https://www.cavd.co.uk/

# What the market research changes

## Do not build “the platform” first

The reusable portion is smaller than it initially sounds:

1. **Vertical config** — questions, compatibility rules, offer types, copy.
2. **Catalogue/offer adapter** — CSV/JSON/manual first.
3. **Advisor UI** — questions → recommendation / quote.
4. **Conversion adapter** — affiliate click, Shopify cart, or lead form.
5. **Analytics** — source, keyword, advisor answers, value, conversion.
6. **Creative generator later** — only after a message/offer converts.

Everything else should remain manual until volume forces automation.

## Best business architecture

The same frontend should support three modes:

- **Lead mode:** garden rooms / installations / local services.
- **Retail mode:** golf / espresso / astronomy.
- **Quote/procurement mode:** commercial coffee / AV / B2B equipment.

That is enough “build once, repackage.”

## The most important formula

For lead/brokerage:

`Break-even CPC = value per closed job × lead-to-close rate × click-to-lead rate`

For ecommerce:

`Break-even CPC = pre-ad contribution/order × click-to-order conversion rate`

Do not buy traffic until the variables can be populated with real commercial terms.

# Final recommendation

**Build and test in this order:**

1. Garden-room lead concierge — lowest capital, clearest proof, fastest conversion test.
2. Golf-simulator advisor — strongest premium consumer retail thesis; validate via affiliate/organic while negotiating trade margin.
3. Commercial coffee equipment advisor — highest B2B transaction values with current reseller + leasing infrastructure.

Run these three off the same frontend. If none produce qualified demand, stop. If one works, deepen only that vertical before adding automation.
