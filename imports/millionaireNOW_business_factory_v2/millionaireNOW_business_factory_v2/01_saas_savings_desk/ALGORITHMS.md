1. Seat waste = unused seats × annual cost / seats.
2. Renewal increase = current annual cost × quoted increase %.
3. Benchmark gap is admitted only when a sourced observation is <=180 days old.
4. To avoid double counting, take max(renewal increase, benchmark gap), then add seat waste.
5. Duplicate categories are review flags only.
6. Priority combines opportunity/spend ratio, renewal urgency, duplicate flag and evidence confidence.
