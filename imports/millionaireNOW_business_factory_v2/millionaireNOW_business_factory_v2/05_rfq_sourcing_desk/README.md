# B2B RFQ Sourcing Desk
Buyer specification in; hard supplier eligibility, capability fit, normalized landed quote comparison and award outcome out. Supplier commission is stored but explicitly excluded from ranking.

Start as a concierge in one ugly sourcing niche: custom packaging, fabricated parts, specialist printing, lab consumables, event hardware, commercial furniture, etc. Buyer can pay a sourcing fee, supplier can pay a disclosed referral fee, or you can negotiate a spread after demand is proven.
