# Q4 2026 Opportunity Map

## Macro facts that matter

- Shopify merchants produced $14.6B of BFCM 2025 sales, up 27% YoY, with an average cart around $115.
- Google and Microsoft both frame 2026 as the first heavily AI-mediated holiday-shopping season.
- Microsoft reports inspiration/custom-gifting queries have roughly tripled YoY and most sale shoppers remain open to discovering new brands.
- Etsy reports custom/made-to-order merchandise was ~30% of marketplace GMS in 2025.
- Google now gives demand forecasts up to ~180 days, meaning product research can be run as a live pipeline rather than intuition.

## My Q4 shortlist for this operating model

### Tier A: launch research immediately
1. Personalized gift outputs that can be fulfilled by POD/local personalization suppliers.
2. Premium pet gift bundles.
3. Hobby / collector organization and display systems.
4. Screen-free adult/family activity gifts.
5. Travel organization bundles.
6. Non-electrical cozy-home gift sets.
7. Hosting / dinner-party accessory bundles.
8. Premium desk/home organization systems.

### Tier B: attractive only with verified supplier/compliance
- projectors,
- styling tools,
- heated apparel,
- battery accessories,
- kitchen devices.

### Tier C: avoid for first low-capital test
- supplements,
- health claims,
- baby/child safety items,
- heavy furniture,
- fragile oversized products,
- trademark-dependent goods.

## Query-first product discovery examples

Instead of asking “what product should I sell?”, search demand patterns:
- gift for [persona]
- best [product type] for [use]
- [product] set
- premium [product]
- [product] for small spaces
- [product] organizer
- [product] storage
- [product] gift set
- [product] under £100 / £200
- [material] [product]
- personalized [occasion] gift
- last minute [occasion] gift
- [product] next day delivery
- [product] UK / Norway / Denmark
- [product] reviews / comparison

The opportunity is often in a *modifier* with high purchase intent, not a mysterious new product.
