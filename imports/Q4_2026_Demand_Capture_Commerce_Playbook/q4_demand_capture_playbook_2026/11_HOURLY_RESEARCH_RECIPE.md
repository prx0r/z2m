# Research Recipe — Repeatable Hourly Intelligence Loop

## Objective
Find **novel, low-upfront-capital demand-capture business methods** being used by current operators, then convert them into testable recipes.

## Search lanes each run

Rotate lanes so the report does not become 20 copies of “run Google Ads.”

### Lane A — operator evidence
- recent YouTube case studies with live dashboards,
- Reddit write-ups,
- X threads from working media buyers/merchants,
- podcasts with store operators,
- Shopify founder interviews.

### Lane B — platform changes
- Google Ads / Merchant Center releases,
- Microsoft Ads retail features,
- Shopify product updates,
- Etsy/Amazon seller changes,
- new AI commerce surfaces,
- payment / checkout improvements.

### Lane C — demand shifts
- Google Trends / demand forecast,
- gift queries,
- seasonality,
- emerging recipient/occasion searches,
- product shortages,
- local-language gaps.

### Lane D — supply shifts
- new supplier warehouses,
- new POD/personalization capabilities,
- wholesalers without strong ecommerce,
- brands adding dropship/dealer programs,
- regional fulfillment providers.

### Lane E — overlooked arbitrage
- same product, different country,
- same product, different buyer persona,
- same product, bundled,
- same demand, faster delivery,
- same demand, better data/Q&A,
- same catalog, Microsoft/free listings,
- same product, B2B instead of B2C,
- same market, local maker instead of China.

## Evidence grading

A — official platform data / company filings / government  
B — named operator with detailed process and verifiable presence  
C — Reddit/self-report with screenshots or numerical detail  
D — generic guru claim / affiliate article  
E — unsourced claim

Never present D/E as proof.

## Novelty rule
A method counts as novel only if at least one changes:
- acquisition channel,
- market,
- buyer,
- offer,
- supply source,
- fulfillment,
- data advantage,
- monetization,
- timing,
- risk structure.

## Output per method
- name,
- source/operator,
- date,
- exact method,
- why it works,
- startup capital,
- first test,
- kill metric,
- scale path,
- biggest risk,
- evidence grade,
- what is genuinely new vs previous reports.

## The master heuristic

**Existing demand + weak execution + cheap fix + measurable intent + low irreversible cost.**
