# 90-Day Q4 2026 Execution Plan

Date context: early September 2026.

## Week 1 — intelligence
- Pull 200 candidates from:
  - Google demand forecasts,
  - Keyword Planner,
  - Google Shopping,
  - Google Ads Transparency Center,
  - Google Holiday 100 historical signals,
  - Etsy trend / personalization signals,
  - Amazon/Etsy bestseller evidence,
  - TikTok/Meta creative libraries,
  - supplier order volume,
  - Reddit buyer complaints.
- Score each with the included scanner.
- Reject products with compliance/IP/shipping red flags.
- Reverse-source the top 30.
- Get real supplier quotes and shipping windows.

## Week 2 — offer engineering
For top 10:
- Compare first Shopping row.
- Screenshot/record:
  - price,
  - seller,
  - review count,
  - delivery,
  - image quality,
  - offer,
  - bundle,
  - returns.
- Identify one moat per product.
- Create 3–5 coherent collections.
- Pick 30–80 initial SKUs.
- Order samples of top 3–5 if quality uncertainty is material.

## Week 3 — store + feed
- Build brand around category/problem, not random general goods.
- Business identity and compliance pages first.
- Build hero pages.
- Feed enrichment.
- Merchant Center setup.
- Resolve every diagnostic.
- Enable free listings.

## Week 4 — first paid test
- Launch small Standard Shopping or feed-only PMax.
- Keep a strict risk cap.
- Search-term review.
- Kill irrelevant traffic.
- Collect first customer objections.
- Improve pages/Q&A.

## October — prove and expand
- Separate winners.
- Expand winning collections horizontally.
- Negotiate supplier pricing.
- Add Microsoft Shopping.
- Add gift-guide pages.
- Add bundles.
- Build email/SMS.
- Move winners to faster/local fulfillment if possible.
- Begin BFCM offer testing before peak CPC inflation.

## November — scale only proven units
- Protect stock and shipping.
- Begin BFCM earlier than the four-day weekend.
- Avoid sitewide discounts that destroy contribution.
- Prefer:
  - bundle savings,
  - free gift,
  - tiered spend,
  - free shipping threshold,
  - early-access offer.
- Raise budgets gradually on proven SKUs.
- Watch Auction Insights / competitor pricing.
- Update delivery cutoffs daily.

## December — deadline arbitrage
The closer to Christmas:
- fast delivery becomes more valuable than lowest price,
- remove products that cannot arrive on time,
- pivot messaging to “arrives before Christmas” only when true,
- promote digital/personalized outputs with instant delivery where applicable,
- emphasize gift cards / printable or digital components for final days.

## January
Do not disappear.
- returns management,
- post-purchase offers,
- self-improvement / organization / fitness-adjacent categories,
- winter evergreen products,
- collect reviews,
- document real profitability,
- decide which store earns a permanent brand investment.
