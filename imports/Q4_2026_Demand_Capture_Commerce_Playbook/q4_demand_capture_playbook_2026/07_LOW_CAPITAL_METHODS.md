## Rule for future research

Every new method should be reduced to five fields:
1. **Demand source** — where buyer intent already exists.
2. **Supply source** — who is already able to fulfill.
3. **Friction** — why incumbents fail the buyer.
4. **Cheap wedge** — what can be improved without inventory.
5. **Scale path** — what gets better after the first 20 orders.

This is the reusable formula for the hourly research task.
