# 12 Experiments to Run Before Q4 Scale

1. **Title A/B by feed label** — search-term-first vs benefit-first.
2. **Main image test** — clean product vs contextual lifestyle image.
3. **Bundle vs single SKU** — same buyer problem, higher AOV.
4. **Free shipping threshold** — test contribution, not only conversion.
5. **Fast-delivery SKU set** — isolate local-warehouse products.
6. **Standard Shopping vs feed-only PMax** — same product group, non-overlapping structure where possible.
7. **Own-brand exclusion test** — measure real prospecting rather than branded leakage.
8. **Microsoft import** — cap spend and compare contribution per click.
9. **Local language market** — one proven collection only.
10. **AI buying-guide page** — comparison/Q&A linked from hero products.
11. **Gift-recipient landing pages** — “for dad,” “for couples,” “for pet owners,” etc.
12. **BFCM offer type** — percentage discount vs bundle/free gift/free shipping threshold.

For every test:
- hypothesis,
- one variable,
- minimum spend/order threshold,
- contribution metric,
- kill condition,
- result,
- follow-up.
