# Product Feed Advantage — Q4 2026

## Why this is more important now

Merchant Center data now powers:
- Shopping ads,
- free listings,
- AI Mode shopping,
- Gemini shopping experiences,
- Lens,
- Images,
- YouTube product surfaces,
- emerging agentic commerce.

In 2026 Google specifically recommends richer conversational product data.

## Feed title formula

Do not keyword-stuff. Put the highest-intent differentiators early.

Examples:

`Primary product term + key attribute + use/persona + size/count`

`Material + product term + functional differentiator + compatibility`

`Personalized + occasion + recipient + product type`

Examples:
- Personalized First Christmas Keepsake — Couple Names + Date
- Modular Trading Card Display Case — UV Resistant — 12 Slab
- Travel Cable Organizer Set — Tech Accessories — 3 Piece
- Premium Dog Travel Kit — Seat Cover + Bowl + Storage Bag

## Product page structure

1. Exact answer to what the product is.
2. Who it is for.
3. Why this version is different.
4. What is included.
5. Dimensions / fit / compatibility.
6. Delivery date and shipping.
7. Returns/warranty.
8. Comparison vs common alternatives.
9. Real reviews.
10. FAQ with natural-language buyer questions.
11. Rich imagery from several angles.
12. Short video if useful.

## Conversational discovery checklist

Write product information that can answer:
- Will this fit X?
- Is this suitable for Y?
- What comes in the box?
- How is it different from Z?
- How quickly can it arrive?
- Is it gift-ready?
- What material is it?
- Can it be personalized?
- What size should I choose?
- Is it compatible with [device/use]?
- What is the return policy?
- Is it good for [persona/occasion]?

This is useful to both humans and AI shopping systems.

## Image standard

Google's 2026 holiday guidance recommends:
- high resolution,
- 1500×1500px+ where practical,
- 3+ additional angles,
- lifestyle context,
- short-form video when useful.

For a low-capital brand, AI can help with:
- background cleanup,
- scene generation,
- infographics,
- comparison diagrams,
- variant visualization.

But do not misrepresent the actual product.
