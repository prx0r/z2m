# Merchant Center / Trust / Compliance Checklist

## Identity
- Legal/business name matches relevant records.
- Domain and store identity are consistent.
- Valid contact information.
- Physical/business address where required/appropriate.
- No fabricated social proof.
- No misleading “official” / “authorized” claims.

## Product truth
- Feed title matches page.
- Price matches page/checkout.
- Availability matches.
- Variants match.
- Images represent actual product.
- No false before/after results.
- No unsupported health claims.
- Genuine GTIN/brand data only.

## Shipping
- Processing time explicit.
- Transit time explicit.
- Country-specific costs.
- Tracking expectation.
- Holiday cutoff policy.
- No “2–3 day shipping” when supplier is 10–14 days.

## Returns
- Return window.
- Eligible condition.
- How to initiate return.
- Return address/process.
- Who pays return shipping.
- Refund processing time.
- Damaged/defective process.

## Store quality
- HTTPS.
- No broken pages.
- Mobile checkout.
- Policies accessible from footer.
- Contact method visible.
- About/business information.
- Product pages complete.
- No placeholder text.
- No contradictory delivery claims.

## Before appeal after a suspension
Do not spam appeals.
1. Audit site/feed/policies.
2. Fix inconsistencies.
3. Re-crawl/re-sync data.
4. Document changes.
5. Request review only when the store is actually compliant.

## UK
Online/distance selling has mandatory information rules. VAT depends on exact seller, goods and import setup. Verify before launch.

## Restricted / high-risk beginner categories
Avoid unless you know the compliance regime:
- supplements,
- medical/wellness claims,
- cosmetics,
- batteries/electrical devices,
- children’s safety products,
- PPE,
- weapons,
- trademark/counterfeit goods.

This file is operational guidance, not legal advice.
