# Anti-Patterns — How This Model Fails

1. **Temu next to you for 1/5 the price.**
   Fix: unique bundle, local stock, service, personalization, compatibility, category expertise.

2. **Fake “brand” with no trust.**
   Fix: actual identity, honest policies, real support, credible content.

3. **1,000 copied SKUs with bad feeds.**
   Fix: start curated, source-first, clean product data, expand only where proven.

4. **Supplier unknown until after first order.**
   Fix: supplier and fulfillment validation before import.

5. **Shipping promise copied from competitor.**
   Fix: real lane-specific shipping data.

6. **Scale on revenue/ROAS only.**
   Fix: contribution after taxes, fees, returns and support.

7. **Hard seasonal deadline + overseas shipping.**
   Fix: local warehouse or stop ads well before cutoff.

8. **Merchant Center suspension after store build.**
   Fix: compliance-first architecture, not policy pages pasted at the end.

9. **Generic AI copy.**
   Fix: factual, product-specific Q&A and comparison information.

10. **AI-generated product images misrepresent product.**
    Fix: use AI for context/cleanup but preserve actual features.

11. **Random multi-niche general store.**
    Fix: category coherence so trust, internal linking and buyer guidance compound.

12. **Course-content dependency.**
    Fix: verify operator advice against official platform docs, live SERPs and your own contribution data.
