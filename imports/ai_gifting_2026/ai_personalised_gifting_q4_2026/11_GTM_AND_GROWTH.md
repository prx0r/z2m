# Go-To-Market and Growth

## Channel order for low capital

1. **SEO / programmatic editorial**
2. **Pinterest organic**
3. **Etsy as demand laboratory**
4. **Google free Merchant listings**
5. **creator seeding / UGC**
6. **email/reminders/referrals**
7. **tiny high-intent Google Search/Shopping tests**
8. paid social only after strong creative/conversion proof

## 1. Search strategy

Target long-tail emotional jobs, not “personalised gifts.”

Bad:
- personalised gifts
- Christmas gifts

Better:
- first Christmas married ornament;
- personalised greyhound Christmas ornament;
- gift for dad who hates everything;
- photo puzzle for grandparents;
- personalised Secret Santa gift coworker;
- custom calendar from family photos.

### Page architecture
`/uk/christmas/for-dad/gardening/`
`/uk/christmas/pets/greyhound/`
`/uk/first-christmas/new-home/`
`/nl/sinterklaas/kind/naamcadeau/`

Only build pages that can show genuinely relevant creative and products.

## 2. Pinterest

Pinterest is particularly aligned:
- planning happens early;
- product is visual;
- gifting is intent-rich;
- aesthetics can map directly into generated product collections.

2026 Pinterest holiday material says visual search grew markedly faster than text search last holiday period and recommends an early, always-on approach.

### Content machine
For every validated product:
- 5 recipient angles;
- 5 art styles;
- 3 product mockups;
- 3 price/bundle angles;
- 2 video/animation variants.

But do not spam duplicates.
Each creative should answer a different intent.

## 3. Etsy as a laboratory

Etsy is valuable because it already contains personalised-gift buyers.

Use it to test:
- titles/phrases;
- prices;
- visual styles;
- recipients;
- products;
- reviews;
- seasonal timing.

Move the durable relationship to your owned brand only in ways that comply with Etsy rules/policies.

Etsy requires AI disclosure for seller-prompted AI creations under its Creativity Standards and disclosure of production partners where applicable.

## 4. Google Merchant / Shopping

Free listings + Shopping can validate exact SKU intent.

Optimize:
- product title with recipient/occasion;
- clear personalized preview;
- shipping deadline;
- review/social proof;
- price.

Google's July 2026 holiday ad guidance emphasizes video-driven product validation and direct checkout-path improvements.

## 5. Creator strategy

Do not pay generic influencers initially.

Seed creators whose audience has a natural recipient identity:
- dog breeds;
- new parents;
- weddings;
- specific hobby communities;
- regional/local;
- teachers;
- grandparent/family;
- travel couples.

Best content:
**photo in → absurdly good physical gift out.**

## 6. Email is the structural advantage

Capture:
- customer birthday;
- recipient name;
- recipient relation;
- recipient birthday;
- anniversary;
- Christmas interest;
- style preferences.

Then send:
- 30 days before;
- 14 days before;
- final safe-delivery deadline.

The email should contain 3 generated concepts, not “shop now.”

## 7. Referral loop

Generated designs are inherently shareable.

After purchase:
“Send this preview to a sibling and let them add a matching gift.”

For family gifts:
- one person creates “family world”;
- others can buy variants;
- creator gets credit.

Amazon's 2026 custom merch includes shareable designs—this is an important behavior to copy and extend.

## 8. Conversion UX

Never open with a catalogue.

Open with:
**“Who are you buying for?”**

Then:
- relation;
- occasion;
- budget;
- tone;
- photo;
- generate.

Show 4 great finished outputs within seconds.

## 9. Black Friday

Do not simply discount the entire site.

Use:
- bundles;
- free card with gift;
- buy 2 ornaments / family set;
- free standard delivery threshold;
- early delivery guarantee;
- subscriber credit.

Protect margin and increase basket.
