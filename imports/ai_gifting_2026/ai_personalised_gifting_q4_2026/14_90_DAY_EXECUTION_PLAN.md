# 90-Day Execution Plan — Starting 5 September 2026

## Constraint
Do not overbuild.

The goal by Black Friday is not “full Moonpig clone.”
It is:
- one excellent creation flow;
- 5–7 products;
- one UK fulfilment route;
- 20–40 validated landing-page hypotheses;
- real conversion data.

# Days 1–7 — prove fulfilment + creative quality

### Supply
- create Prodigi account/API;
- create Gelato account/API as backup;
- order samples:
  - card;
  - ornament;
  - puzzle;
  - mug;
  - magazine/book;
  - calendar.

### Product
Build one mobile flow:
1. recipient
2. occasion
3. tone
4. photo
5. generate 4 concepts
6. select product
7. preview
8. checkout

### Quality
Test 50 recipient briefs manually.
Score:
- “would I gift this?”
- face likeness;
- exact names;
- humour;
- aesthetics;
- print readiness.

Kill styles that look like generic AI.

# Days 8–14 — launch demand lab

Create 20 hypothesis pages around:
- pet Christmas;
- first Christmas;
- new home;
- new baby;
- couple;
- grandparents;
- Secret Santa;
- “our year”;
- puzzle;
- ornament.

Publish:
- Pinterest;
- Etsy subset;
- Merchant feed;
- organic SEO.

Spend only tiny budgets on exact/high-intent searches.

# Days 15–30 — find first winning wedge

Track:
- landing CTR;
- generate-start;
- completed generation;
- save/share;
- add-to-cart;
- checkout;
- contribution;
- customer support.

Target:
find at least **one recipient × product × creative style** that reliably gets:
- saves/shares;
- checkout intent;
- acceptable contribution.

# October — expand winners, not catalogue

If “pet medieval ornament” wins:
- dog breeds;
- cats;
- memorial;
- family set;
- wrapping paper attach;
- card attach;
- 6 visual directions.

Do not add 50 unrelated SKUs.

### Build reminder layer
After checkout:
- recipient birthday;
- anniversary;
- save style;
- Christmas next year.

### Creator seeding
Send physical samples to 20–50 micro-creators in the winning niche.

# November — commercial scale

### Black Friday offer
Bundle:
- hero gift;
- free/cheap card;
- second matching gift;
- delivery messaging.

### Paid
Scale only ads with measured contribution.
Separate:
- brand;
- exact product intent;
- retargeting;
- creator winners.

### Ops
Daily:
- provider latency;
- late risk;
- customer support;
- refund/reprint;
- delivery cutoff.

# December — deadline engine

Dynamically alter merchandising by destination:
- “arrives before Christmas” products;
- fastest products first;
- hide risky SKUs;
- digital reveal fallback after cutoff.

# Success thresholds

By Jan 1, you want evidence for:
1. at least 1–3 strong product wedges;
2. organic/creator content that produces purchases;
3. repeat/recipient/reminder signup behavior;
4. reliable provider SLA;
5. customer willingness to pay for AI-directed personalization;
6. enough margin to fund the next acquisition loop.

# Failure interpretation

If it fails, distinguish:
- no demand;
- bad creative;
- bad product;
- slow UX;
- price;
- shipping;
- trust;
- channel mismatch.

Do not conclude “AI gifts don't work” from a weak template store.
