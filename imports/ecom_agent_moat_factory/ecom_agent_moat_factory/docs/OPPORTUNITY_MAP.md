## Best second-order products

### Support-to-Merchandising Intelligence
Mine calls, chats, returns and claims for repeated unanswered questions, compatibility confusion and misleading expectations. Automatically propose PDP/FAQ/feed changes, then measure whether the question/return rate falls.

Why this gets a moat: every merchant builds a proprietary dataset linking **customer language → product content change → downstream conversion/return outcome**.

### Supplier Quality Oracle
Aggregate claims, replacements, delivery exceptions and returns by supplier, SKU, batch and fulfillment route. Use it internally for GeoCommerce, then potentially sell it to merchants sourcing similar products.

### AI-Human Relay Layer
Many merchants do not want “AI replaces support.” They want AI to answer 24/7, gather context and reliably get a human involved. A standardized relay product can create a Gorgias/Zendesk ticket, schedule callback, attach transcript, customer/order context, predicted intent and recommended next action.

This is less flashy than a chatbot and potentially stickier.
