# 2026–2031 Growth Thesis

## What gets cheaper

- speech recognition and text-to-speech;
- multilingual voice quality;
- LLM inference;
- catalog ingestion;
- workflow generation;
- implementation through coding agents.

Inworld already publishes realtime TTS from $15/1M characters on-demand and STT at $0.15/hour, with materially lower committed rates. Its worked July 2026 model puts one complete cascaded voice stack around $0.013/conversation-minute on demand under stated assumptions. Retell commoditizes the full managed stack at $0.07–$0.31/min.

**Implication:** voice itself will not remain the moat.

## What gets more valuable

### 1. Action reliability
Merchants will pay for an agent that can safely *resolve* an issue, not merely answer it.

### 2. Merchant-specific policy
Refund threshold, replacement rules, VIP treatment, warranty exceptions, delivery remediation and escalation are business logic. Capturing these as versioned policies creates switching cost.

### 3. Outcome data
The useful proprietary dataset is:
`state → recommended action → action taken → cost → customer outcome → revenue/margin outcome`.

### 4. Cross-channel identity
One customer should be recognized across voice, chat, email, SMS, orders, returns and claims. That requires integration work and becomes sticky.

### 5. Vertical playbooks
A water-softener support agent needs different logic from a coffee-equipment agent. Reusable vertical troubleshooting and selling trees are defensible because they are earned from real outcomes.

## Expected category evolution

### 2026–2027: installation era
Merchants buy “AI agent” products and test channel automation. Many horizontal wrappers compete on price.

### 2027–2029: resolution era
Winning vendors measure completed commerce actions, retained revenue and avoided operational cost. Vendors without deep integrations are squeezed.

### 2029–2031: agent operating layer
Customer journeys increasingly begin in AI channels. Merchant-side agents coordinate with shopping agents, logistics systems, suppliers, payments and humans. The valuable software is policy + transaction context + evidence + outcomes.

## Strategic position

Do not aim to be the cheapest voice API reseller. Own **ecommerce resolution primitives** and allow different voice/chat providers underneath them.
