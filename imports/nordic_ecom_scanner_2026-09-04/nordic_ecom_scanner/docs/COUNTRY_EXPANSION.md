# Extensible Country Expansion Strategy

## Country deployment contract

A new country is not “supported” merely because the store can display its currency. Before enabling paid acquisition, populate:

- language/locale;
- VAT/GST rate and import scheme;
- preferred payment methods;
- target and maximum delivery time;
- customer return expectations;
- Google Shopping/CSS availability;
- local keyword-provider location/language identifiers;
- expected payment fee;
- consumer-law checklist;
- prohibited/high-friction product categories;
- local support and returns workflow.

Then run 100+ localized commercial queries before ranking niches.

## Shared-store vs separate-store decision

Use a **shared canonical backend** but isolate market presentation and measurement.

A candidate winner is portable when:
- supplier SKU/compliance can legally serve both countries;
- landed-cost spread still works;
- local Shopping query intent exists;
- local competitors leave a merchandising gap;
- delivery/returns remain acceptable.

Do not assume that a winning Norwegian product will win in Denmark simply because the countries are nearby.

## Rollout rule

1. Prove niche in one market at measured positive contribution.
2. Translate/search-research the same SKU family in adjacent market.
3. Compare local CPC, competitor price and merchant concentration.
4. Re-score using that market's VAT/payment/delivery assumptions.
5. Reuse assets only where the product representation remains exact and culturally appropriate.
6. Localize feed/store/support.
7. Run a fresh bounded acquisition test.
