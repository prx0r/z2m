# Executive summary

## Verdict

The thesis is legitimate **only after removing the “AI content farm” framing**.

The profitable pattern is:

**recurring demand -> structured/verified data -> decision tool -> commercial action -> automated maintenance**

The bad pattern is:

**find keywords -> generate 500 thin pages -> add affiliate links -> hope**

Google's 2026 guidance explicitly warns against scaled low-value AI pages, and AI Overviews are intercepting more generic informational clicks. This makes proprietary/current data, tools, comparisons, listings, alerts, quote requests, and marketplaces more valuable than generic prose.

## Strongest validated examples

- **JobBoardSearch:** Stripe-verified, $2,215 last 30 days and $96,466 all-time as of late Aug 2026. Monetizes sponsored slots, listing upgrades, ads/CPC and services.
- **TrustMRR:** Stripe-verified, $39,776 last 30 days, $19,036 MRR and $317,592 all-time on 4 Sep 2026. Monetizes listing tiers, add-ons, sponsorship, paid data, buyer alerts and a 3% marketplace closing fee.
- **AffiliList:** Stripe-verified, $117 last 30 days and $2,072 all-time. A tiny narrow directory charging for submissions.
- **Counterexamples:** DirectoryHunt (~$80 all-time) and StartupDirectory.net (~$120 all-time) prove that directory software alone does not make money.
- **Real Work From Anywhere:** founder reported >$5K/mo for three months after years of building; roughly $6 hosting. Useful evidence that aggregation can become low-maintenance, but not quickly.
- **Nomad List + Remote OK:** long-running first-party evidence of seven-figure annualized revenue from structured data/community/job-board assets.
