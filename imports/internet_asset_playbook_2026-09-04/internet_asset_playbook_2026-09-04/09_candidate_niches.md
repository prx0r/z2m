## Top three for you specifically

### 1. AISec evidence directory

This is the highest strategic value because every research record can simultaneously:

- rank/index;
- become a public research asset;
- feed your audit tooling;
- identify potential consulting clients;
- create vendor relationships;
- build a proprietary corpus.

The site is not “10 best agent security tools.” It is “show me tools/consultants that can test **this exact agent stack**, with evidence.”

### 2. Bittensor operator intelligence

You already care about the exact data users need to decide whether to enter a subnet. A good page is a live operator decision sheet, not an article.

Potential page family:

`/subnet/<id>/mine`

with:

- active miners;
- reward distribution;
- registration economics;
- hardware/API requirements;
- public miner repos;
- validator scoring mechanism;
- observed update date;
- historical snapshots;
- action checklist.

### 3. Premium buyer “quote desks”

This fits your broader commerce thesis better than generic dropshipping. Pick one confusing, £500–£20K purchase and become the buyer’s decision interface.

Examples:

- commercial espresso setups;
- home sauna packages;
- small hotel laundry equipment;
- rural backup connectivity;
- premium water filtration;
- specialist workshop equipment.

The site can route the lead to suppliers rather than taking inventory risk.
