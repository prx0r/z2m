# 6. 90-day launch plan

## Days 1–3 — choose a money loop, not a niche

Pick three candidate loops and prove them manually.

For each candidate:

1. collect 50 real records;
2. write one perfect decision page;
3. identify 20 potential partners;
4. find at least one affiliate/referral program or define a direct lead price;
5. make a simple landing page;
6. ask 10 target users if the page saves them meaningful time/money;
7. ask 10 vendors if they would pay for leads/visibility.

**Kill two. Keep one.**

## Days 4–7 — pre-monetize

Do not automate outbound yet.

Offer one of:

- founding sponsor: £49–£199;
- enhanced profile: £29–£99 one-time;
- first 10 leads free, then £X per qualified lead;
- concierge buyer shortlist: £29–£99;
- lifetime founding alert plan.

Target: **first £1 before major engineering.**

## Week 2 — build the smallest useful database

Ship:

- 30–100 verified records;
- filters;
- detail pages;
- update timestamp;
- evidence/source field;
- one transaction CTA;
- sitemap;
- analytics;
- Search Console;
- email capture.

Do not publish hundreds of keyword permutations.

## Weeks 3–4 — distribution before SEO compounding

Every week:

- answer 5–10 relevant community questions with the tool only where genuinely useful;
- publish one original data insight from your database;
- onboard 5 vendors/providers;
- ask for one backlink from each verified provider profile;
- add one free utility/calculator;
- contact relevant newsletters/podcasts/communities;
- improve the top 10 pages based on real usage.

## Month 2 — add the moat

Choose one:

- verification;
- price history;
- response-time tracking;
- historical outcomes;
- reviews from completed transactions;
- data feeds;
- unique score;
- alerts.

This is where the project stops being cloneable in an afternoon.

## Month 2 monetization target

You want evidence of at least one of:

- 3 paying vendors;
- 10 affiliate conversions;
- 10 qualified leads;
- 20 paid subscribers;
- one productized-service client;
- repeat sponsor.

If none occur, revise the money loop before adding pages.

## Month 3 — scale only proven page families

A page family earns the right to scale if:

- pages are indexed reliably;
- impressions grow;
- CTR is not collapsing;
- visitors interact with the tool/data;
- at least one conversion path exists;
- each new page has genuinely unique structured data.

Then expand 10 -> 30 -> 100 pages, measuring at each step.

## Autopilot target state

Only after the loop works should an agent take over:

- stale-record detection;
- source checking;
- change summaries;
- page-update drafts;
- sitemap regeneration;
- broken-link checks;
- analytics summary;
- partner lead discovery;
- outreach drafts;
- weekly revenue report.

Human approval remains required for:

- publication of novel factual claims;
- cold email sending;
- affiliate applications;
- pricing changes;
- refunds;
- deleting vendors;
- legal/compliance claims.
