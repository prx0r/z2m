# Prompt: partner outreach draft

Draft, do not send, a short partner email for a provider already included in our directory/database.

Use only verified facts.

The email should:

- tell them exactly where they are listed;
- mention one useful metric only if we actually have it (views/clicks/leads);
- ask them to verify/correct their profile for free;
- offer one clear paid option only after the free value is obvious;
- avoid fake scarcity, inflated traffic claims, or manipulative language;
- include an unsubscribe/no-more-contact line for cold outreach where appropriate.

Possible paid offer:

- featured placement;
- category sponsorship;
- qualified-lead plan;
- verified/enhanced profile;
- newsletter placement.

Never send automatically. Human approval is required.
