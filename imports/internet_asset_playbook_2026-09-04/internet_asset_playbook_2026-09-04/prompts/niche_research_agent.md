# Prompt: niche research agent

You are evaluating a candidate niche for a low-capital internet asset.

Your job is **not** to produce content. Your job is to decide whether a useful data product can make money.

For the niche, collect:

1. 20 real user queries or complaint/request patterns.
2. 50 entities/providers/products that could populate the database.
3. The primary source URL for each entity.
4. Which fields users need to compare before acting.
5. Which fields change over time.
6. Ten potential paying vendors/partners.
7. All obvious affiliate/referral/listing programs you can verify.
8. The likely high-intent page families.
9. Existing competitors and what data/UX they lack.
10. Data-access constraints, terms, and scraping/API risks.

Then score 0–5 on:

- repeated intent;
- economic value;
- fragmentation;
- freshness/data moat;
- monetization depth;
- distribution wedge;
- defensibility.

Reject the niche if:

- it needs thin keyword pages;
- most value is generic prose;
- you cannot identify three monetization paths;
- primary data cannot be sourced legally/reliably;
- there is no useful single page before scale.

Output:

- score /35;
- strongest money loop;
- first perfect page to build;
- first 20 vendors/users to interview;
- 7-day validation experiment;
- reasons not to build it.
