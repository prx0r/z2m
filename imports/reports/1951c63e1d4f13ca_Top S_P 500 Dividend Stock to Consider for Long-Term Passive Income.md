History of the Company
Tractor Supply Company (TSCO) was founded in 1938 as a small mail-order business providing tractor parts and agricultural equipment. Its founder, Charles Schmidt, recognized the growing demand for affordable, high-quality parts among farmers and ranchers. The success of the catalog quickly led to the opening of the first retail store in Minnesota in 1939.
Over the following decades, the company expanded rapidly, establishing new stores across the United States. In 1959, Tractor Supply went public, securing investment for further growth. During the 1970s and 1980s, the company strengthened its reputation as a leading retailer catering to farmers, rural homeowners, and agricultural businesses.
As technology evolved and consumer habits shifted, Tractor Supply adapted by launching e-commerce operations and optimizing logistics. In 2004, the company relocated its headquarters to Brentwood, Tennessee. Throughout the 2020s, it continued expanding its store network, investing in digital transformation, and enhancing its loyalty program, which now includes millions of members.
Today, Tractor Supply Company operates over 2,200 stores in 49 U.S. states, serving more than 23 million customers. It remains the largest retail chain dedicated to farmers, ranchers, rural lifestyle enthusiasts, and pet owners, offering a diverse selection of products for agriculture, home improvement, gardening, and animal care.
