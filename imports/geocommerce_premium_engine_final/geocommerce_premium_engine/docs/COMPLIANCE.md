# Compliance and trust gates

This is an engineering checklist, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Reject by default
- regulated/medical products without specialist review;
- health/medical claims unsupported by appropriate evidence;
- child-safety critical goods;
- uncertified electrical products for the destination market;
- branded products without resale authorization where needed;
- counterfeit/ambiguous-origin stock;
- supplier copy with unverifiable certifications;
- fake reviews/testimonials;
- generated media that misrepresents the SKU.

## Required product truth
- HS code and origin before duty automation;
- warranty length and responsible party;
- returns address/process;
- exact dimensions/weight;
- variant identifiers/GTIN when available;
- stock timestamp;
- delivery service and estimate;
- electrical/safety certifications where applicable.

## Google Merchant hygiene
Keep landing page/feed language and currency consistent. Do not create duplicate listings merely to gain unfair traffic advantage. Product titles should maximize truthful relevance, not keyword density at the expense of accuracy.
