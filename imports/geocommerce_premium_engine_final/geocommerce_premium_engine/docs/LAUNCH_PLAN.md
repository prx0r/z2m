# Launch plan: low-capital path

## Phase 0 — credentials and substrate
- Google Ads manager/customer + developer token/OAuth.
- Merchant Center account.
- Shopify dev/store or headless checkout.
- Optional DataForSEO + SerpApi.
- CJ token; apply for AutoDS API only if useful.
- Runway or Gemini/Veo for media after product shortlist.

## Phase 1 — scan without stores
For 20 category hypotheses × 8 countries:
- generate local keyword clusters;
- pull historical metrics;
- collect supplier candidates;
- normalize economics to local currency;
- reject unsafe/low-margin/slow products;
- shortlist top 30 `product × country` opportunities.

## Phase 2 — supplier verification
For top 10:
- exact SKU/GTIN/MPN;
- dealer authorization if branded;
- sample order;
- warranty/returns route;
- shipping cost/time to each market;
- stock-feed availability;
- certifications and HS/origin.

## Phase 3 — compile free-listing store
Launch only 1–3 coherent category stores, each to 1–2 markets. Generate:
- local PDPs;
- Merchant feed;
- advisor;
- support/human handoff;
- SEO/comparison/buying guides;
- free listings.

## Phase 4 — evidence before scaling
Observe free-listing impressions/clicks. Paid tests start at a controlled budget for opportunities with sufficient CPC headroom. Do not ask PMax to discover whether gross economics exist.

## Kill rules
- supplier cannot prove warranty/returns;
- delivery > 14 days for a product positioned as premium unless buyer explicitly expects custom/import lead time;
- contribution margin < 28% before ads;
- break-even CPC < 1.3× observed CPC after real click data;
- severe Merchant disapprovals;
- return/damage rate destroys contribution;
- support questions reveal specification uncertainty the supplier cannot resolve.

## Scale rules
- 20+ qualified paid clicks and strong engagement can justify continued test, but do not infer conversion rate from tiny samples;
- after conversions, replace assumed CVR with observed Bayesian/conservative estimates;
- negotiate supplier pricing only after evidence of demand;
- move winning SKU from public dropship source toward direct dealer/private supplier/stock position.
