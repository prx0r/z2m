## Ideal product fingerprint
- selling price 250–2,500 local equivalent;
- 35%+ contribution margin before ads after VAT/tax, payment, shipping, return and warranty reserves;
- break-even CPC at least 1.5× observed/forecast CPC before paid test;
- <= 7–10 day delivery preferred for premium positioning;
- low sizing ambiguity;
- meaningful differences buyers need help understanding;
- 3+ high-quality reference images or ability to order a sample;
- warranty >= 12 months, ideally 24+;
- supplier inventory/status available programmatically or reliably;
- no regulatory or brand-authorization ambiguity.
