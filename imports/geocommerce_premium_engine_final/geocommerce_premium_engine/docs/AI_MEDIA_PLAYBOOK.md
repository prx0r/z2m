# AI media playbook

## Goal
Turn supplier/sample product imagery into premium visual merchandising cheaply while preserving truth.

## Pipeline
1. Acquire supplier images plus at least one real sample for any product that reaches paid-test status.
2. Store reference images and exact verified geometry/features.
3. Generate three core assets:
   - 5–8s studio orbit;
   - use-case scene showing scale/context;
   - macro craftsmanship/detail shot.
4. Generate country-language overlays separately; do not ask the video model to render critical small text/specs.
5. Human QA against real product before publishing.
6. Track conversion impact by asset ID.

## Providers
- Runway Product Ad recipe: purpose-built from one or more product photos.
- Runway Product UGC recipe: useful after the product is validated, not as fake customer testimony.
- Veo 3.1: reference-image video generation; up to three references can help preserve product identity.

## Hard rule
Generated product demos are merchandising, not evidence. The real sample/spec sheet is evidence. If a generated clip shows a capability that cannot be proven from product truth, reject the clip.
