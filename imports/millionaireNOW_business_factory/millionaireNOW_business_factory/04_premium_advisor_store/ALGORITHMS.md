# Algorithms — Premium Advisor Store

## Two launch modes
1. `affiliate`: truly near-zero working capital; send qualified buyers to approved partner links.
2. `dropship_quote`: capture intent, manually verify supplier stock/cost/terms, then quote. This preserves hard gross margin without pretending supplier settlement timing is risk-free.

## Product ranking
`46% fit + 18% value + 12% price freshness + 12% warranty + 12% modeled margin quality`.

Affiliate commission never changes buyer fit. In direct-sale mode, margin is a viability filter, not a reason to lie about suitability.

## Contribution margin
`price - supplier_cost - shipping - processor_fee - expected_return_reserve`.

Do not scale a SKU until contribution remains positive after refunds, damaged shipments, support time and acquisition cost.

## Best categories
Complex, high-consideration products where buyers need comparison help and several direct supplier/affiliate programs exist: home wellness/saunas, ergonomic office equipment, massage chairs, premium outdoor gear, power stations, specialist coffee equipment. Avoid regulated/high-liability categories until legal and insurance requirements are clear.
