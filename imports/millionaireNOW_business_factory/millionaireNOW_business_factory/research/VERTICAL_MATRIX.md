# Vertical opportunity matrix

Scores are directional launch priorities, not market-size forecasts.

| Vertical | Best kernel | Monetization evidence | Complexity | Competition | Template fit | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home saunas | Advisor + Compare | direct programs paying 6–10% / four-figure examples | medium | medium | excellent | **A+** |
| Massage chairs | Advisor + Compare | current 5% affiliate program; high AOV | medium | medium | excellent | **A** |
| Home inspection / snagging | Match | established home-service lead economics | low | medium | excellent | **A** |
| Commercial cleaning | Match + Outbound | recurring B2B contracts; appointment value | low | medium | excellent | **A** |
| MSP comparison | Match + Outbound | high customer LTV and paid meeting market | medium | high | excellent | **A-** |
| Standing desks | Advisor + Compare | current affiliate programs up to 10% | low | high | excellent | **B+** |
| Portable power stations | Advisor + Compare | active affiliate market; specification-heavy | medium | high | excellent | **B+** |
| SaaS / SEO tools | Compare | $100–$300/sale examples; mature affiliate demand | low | very high | excellent | **B** unless extremely narrow |
| Solar/battery installer | Match | very valuable leads | high | very high | excellent | **B**, later |
| Generic “AI visibility audit” | Audit | emerging demand | low | rising quickly | excellent | **B as standalone; A as funnel** |

## Selection rule
A new vertical needs at least four of these five:
1. a customer spends >£500 or has high recurring LTV
2. buyer has a confusing decision / quote process
3. multiple suppliers or service providers exist
4. a lead/referral/sale has a visible monetary value
5. niche can be expressed mostly as configuration + data
