# 30-day execution plan

## Days 1–3 — find the artifact
- choose 3 candidate verticals from the engine;
- conduct 20 short interviews total;
- collect 10 real redacted artifacts/screenshots/exports;
- quantify current manual minutes and error rate;
- pick the narrowest recurring task with obvious economic value.

Deliverable: `problem.md` containing exact input, exact output, current process, cost, frequency, buyer and existing alternatives.

## Days 4–6 — concierge product
- manually transform/monitor/perform the workflow;
- deliver results by email/Sheets/Slack;
- charge first customer;
- do not build account management yet.

Pass condition: one customer pays and asks to use it again.

## Days 7–12 — deterministic core
Build the smallest reliable pipeline:
- upload/ingest;
- parsing/normalization;
- validation rules;
- correction UI or simple review table;
- export/action;
- audit log.

AI can propose mappings/extractions; deterministic validation should catch obvious bad output.

## Days 13–16 — billing and delivery
- Stripe/Paddle/other lawful processor;
- one paid tier;
- usage counter if relevant;
- email/webhook delivery;
- simple admin screen;
- error reporting.

## Days 17–20 — distribution asset
Pick only one:
- 10 exact-search pages built around real input formats;
- app marketplace integration;
- browser extension listing;
- niche community/tutorial partnership;
- consultant/reseller partner page.

Do not mass-publish generic AI content.

## Days 21–24 — integrations from real requests
Add only the most repeated requested target/source.

## Days 25–27 — proof
Generate:
- before/after workflow video;
- measured time saved;
- supported formats list;
- failure/edge-case policy;
- customer quote if permitted.

## Days 28–30 — decision
Continue only if at least two of these are true:
- 3 paying customers;
- £250+ MRR or strong usage revenue;
- >50% of trial users repeat the workflow;
- customer offers referrals/intros;
- clear exact-search demand is emerging;
- average support <15 minutes/customer/month after onboarding.

Otherwise reuse the engine and transplant another proven recipe.
