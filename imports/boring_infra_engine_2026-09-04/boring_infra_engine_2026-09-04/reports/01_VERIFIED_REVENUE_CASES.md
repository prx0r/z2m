## Notes on selected economics

### 1Lookup
Official pricing starts around **$99/month** and scales to **$1,999/month**, using a shared credit balance across multiple lookup types. This matters because it monetizes *integration simplicity* as much as raw data.

### Web3Forms
The user does not need to deploy backend code. That is the entire primitive: a form POST endpoint, spam handling, email delivery and paid limits. The lesson is that “tiny” products are not necessarily tiny markets.

### Synta
The page reports **211 active subscriptions**, a price around **$34/month**, and claims 300+ businesses use it daily. The key is that it knows a specific workflow deeply enough to create/edit/test/repair n8n automations.

### Groups Watcher
Official pages show a **$199/month** professional tier for 10 groups and much higher done-for-you local lead-gen pricing. This is important: the buyer is paying for speed-to-intent and lead coverage, not data storage.

### OpenAlternative
Official advertising tiers were roughly **$147/$297/$597 per month**, supported by a site claiming ~300k monthly pageviews and 12k newsletter subscribers. The monetizer is the vendor that wants distribution.

### JobBoardSearch
Its listing page offers free inclusion and paid visibility/distribution upgrades. Testimonials explicitly describe it as a meaningful traffic source for job boards. Again, suppliers pay to reach an audience accumulated on the demand side.

### BankConv
The product supports **1,000+ banks** and multiple export formats. Its founder's marketplace note states roughly **$900 MRR and 95% margins**, plus a REST API, Google Sheets add-on and Chrome extension. Profit is a founder claim, but revenue is payment-processor verified.
