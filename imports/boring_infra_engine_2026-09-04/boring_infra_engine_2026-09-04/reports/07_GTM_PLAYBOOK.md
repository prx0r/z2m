# GTM for boring infrastructure

## 1. Exact-intent SEO beats generic content

The strongest boring tools map to queries such as:
- “convert [specific statement] to Xero CSV”
- “[specific portal] export to Excel”
- “[incumbent] webhook”
- “[incumbent] bulk update”
- “[trade] quote template/import”
- “[source] tender alerts”

Each page should contain a real example, supported inputs, exact output, limitations and a working free sample—not generic AI prose.

## 2. Incumbent ecosystems are distribution

A tiny add-on can ride a huge installed base. Build where users already are:
- official app marketplaces;
- extensions;
- OAuth integrations;
- public templates;
- community how-to content.

## 3. Sell saved labor, not AI

Weak: “AI-powered invoice intelligence.”  
Strong: “Turn 200 supplier invoices into job-coded Xero bills in 8 minutes.”

## 4. Use a free primitive to create habit

Examples from the successful patterns:
- free form submission allowance;
- free lookups/credits;
- free directory listing;
- free first pages/documents;
- free monitor for one source.

The free tier should expose the recurring job, not give away unrelated toys.

## 5. Channel partners matter disproportionately

For vertical utilities, one bookkeeper/agency/consultant can bring 10–100 end clients. Build:
- multi-client workspace;
- bulk import;
- white-label output;
- partner discount;
- consolidated billing;
- audit trail.

## 6. Deliver into the user's existing workflow

Email, Slack, Teams, webhook, Google Sheets, CSV, Xero, QuickBooks, CRM. Avoid requiring a daily dashboard visit unless the dashboard itself is the job.

## 7. Pricing heuristics

- Saves 1 hour/month for a £30/hour user → £5–£15/month may work, but not exciting.
- Saves 5 hours/month for a £50/hour professional → £49–£99/month is rational.
- Finds one £5k job every few months → £99–£499/month can be rational.
- Replaces multiple APIs → price as subscription + usage/credits.
- Serves agencies/practices → price per workspace or included clients, then overage.

## 8. Geo-native expansion

The same workflow can be transplanted country-by-country where:
- incumbent systems differ;
- document formats differ;
- language/local terminology blocks global competitors;
- payment willingness remains high.

Do not simply translate UI. Localize integrations, outputs, tax/document naming, search pages and support content.
