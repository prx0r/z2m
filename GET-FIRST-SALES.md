# How to Get the First Sales

---

## The Execution Order

### 1. Etsy as Demand Laboratory (NOW)

- Etsy gives 15 free Marketplace Insights searches/week
- Use queries to decide which wrappers to launch
- Create separate listing per real intent (not one "AI gifts" listing)
- Start with 15-20 listings powered by 4-5 engines
- Etsy 2026 data: personalization, keepsakes, whimsical, "just because" gifting

### 2. Meta/Instagram First (NOT Google)

Personalized products are visually legible. Night Sky reports:
- +36% CTR, +75% conversion, +61% ROAS from creative format testing
- Authentic recipient reactions > synthetic content
- Single-product messaging > brand messaging

**Ad examples:**
- 3 photos of Dad → THE DAD TIMES
- Dog photo → medieval king → ornament → calendar
- Texts/photos → relationship card deck
- Grandad answered 12 questions → hardcover book about his life

### 3. Pinterest NOW (before Q4)

- 68% of users say Pinterest helps holiday decisions
- Visual searches grew 3× faster than text search
- Upload catalogue + tracking before peak season
- 5-10 Pins per core product
- Personalized magazines, gallery walls, pet transformations, ornaments

### 4. Google for Bottom of Funnel

NOT to introduce the concept. "Personalized gifts" is expensive and saturated.

Google works when the query describes the exact output:
- personalized family newspaper gift
- custom pet portrait ornament
- personalized recipe book grandma
- custom anniversary crossword

### 5. Occasion Capture from Day One

After creating a gift, ask permission to save:
> "Alice — birthday 17 Feb — partner — likes coffee — anniversary 8 June"

Moonpig: 113m occasion reminders. Night Sky: 40% YoY repeat purchase increase.

**Next February:** "We made three new things for Alice's birthday." Not "20% off."

### 6. Let Etsy Buy Trust While You Build Trust

- Etsy gives reviews, search traffic, buyer familiarity
- Your site gets the richer "make something for them" experience
- Below $10K annual: Offsite Ads optional (15% attribution fee)
- Price Etsy SKUs to absorb this

### 7. Creative Factory

Don't advertise "Star at Night — extraordinary personalized gifts." Nobody knows what that means.

**Run:**
- "I turned 14 years of family photos into Mum's own Sunday newspaper."
- "I uploaded one picture of my dog and it made his entire Christmas collection."
- "Our WhatsApp messages became a card game about our relationship."

**Single-product messaging > brand messaging.**

---

## The USP (refined)

> **Give me the person, not the product. I'll invent the products.**

Not a gift finder that recommends manufactured items. A system that takes the person and generates 4 completely different gift ideas personalized to them before they commit.

That's what Moonpig, Etsy, Wonderbly, and Night Sky don't do central.

---

## The Creative Factory Pipeline

```
CUSTOMER INPUT (3 photos + 5 answers)
    ↓
AI GENERATES (4 completely different concepts)
    ↓
CUSTOMER PICKS FAVOURITE
    ↓
PRODUCTION (Prodigi/Printify)
    ↓
DELIVERY
    ↓
OCCASION CAPTURE ("Save Alice's birthday?")
    ↓
RECURRING ("We made new things for Alice's birthday")
```

**The AI advantage isn't just generation. It's the ability to show 4 personalized options before the customer commits.**

That's fundamentally different from a gift finder.

---

## The Advertising Play

Run ONE astonishing product per ad. Let the brand emerge from repeated exposure.

**Ad 1:** "I turned 14 years of family photos into Mum's own Sunday newspaper."
**Ad 2:** "I uploaded one picture of my dog and it made his entire Christmas collection."
**Ad 3:** "Our WhatsApp messages became a card game about our relationship."
**Ad 4:** "Grandad answered 12 questions. We turned them into a hardcover book about his life."

The Star at Night logo sits quietly underneath.

**Single-product messaging worked better for Wonderbly than brand messaging.**

## The Priority

1. Etsy listings live (15-20, each with separate intent)
2. Pinterest pins (5-10 per product, running now)
3. Meta/Instagram creative factory (single-product ads)
4. Google for exact-output queries (bottom of funnel)
5. Occasion capture from day one
