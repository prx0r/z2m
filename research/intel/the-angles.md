# The Angles — Where Money Hides

---

## Angle 1: The Stupid People Thesis

**Sell certainty to people who don't know what they don't know.**

The market signal: people pay for things they could technically do themselves but won't.

| What They Could Do | What They Actually Buy | Price |
|-------------------|----------------------|-------|
| Google "how to renovate kitchen" | Kitchen Renovation Pre-Flight Pack | £39 |
| Read Etsy reviews themselves | Personalized pet psychic reading | $40 |
| Fill out a tax form | Tax optimization report | £29 |
| Research golf swing on YouTube | Break-90 personalized plan | $39 |
| Compare contractor quotes themselves | Quote auditor + red flag report | £49 |
| Write their own CV | AI-optimized CV + cover letter | £29 |
| Research moving abroad | Relocation blueprint | £39 |
| Read Airbnb host guides | Guest experience generator | $49 |

**The pattern:** The information is free. The curation, personalization, and packaging is what they pay for.

**Why it works:** People don't buy information. They buy the feeling that something thought specifically about them.

## Angle 2: Personalization at Scale

**AI makes 1:1 personalization economically viable at volume.**

### The 4 Engines (from asymmetric gift factory)

```
1. STORY ENGINE
   facts/photos/voice → book/comic/newspaper/yearbook
   
2. PUZZLE ENGINE  
   facts → crossword/quiz/bingo/scavenger hunt
   
3. DECISION ENGINE
   documents/data → comparison/audit/plan
   
4. CARD WRAPPER
   any digital artifact → physical card + QR + POD gift
```

### These become hundreds of listings:

### Key insight from the pack:

> "The highest-leverage business is not a catalogue of unrelated ebooks. Build 4 reusable engines."

## Angle 4: The "Boring" Gold Mine

**Boring products with high search intent and weak competitors.**

From the gameplan:
- Home organizers
- Boot dryers
- Cable management
- Plant care kits
- Car detailing
- Packing cubes

**Why boring wins:**
- Low competition (nobody wants to make content about cable management)
- High search intent (people searching "cable management desk" want to buy NOW)
- Evergreen (not seasonal)
- Easy to bundle/upsell
- Low returns
- Google rewards specificity

## Angle 5: The AI Creative Arbitrage

**Same product, 100 different angles, let data decide.**

From the gameplan transcript:
- Product at $39.99 → 2.5% conversion
- Same product at $79.99 → 5% conversion
- Double price = double conversion = 4× revenue per visitor

**AI makes testing cheap:**
- 3 hooks × 3 videos × 3 images × 2 landing pages = 90 positions
- Cost per test: ~$5-20 in AI generation
- Let Google Ads data decide winners
- Scale winners, kill losers

## Angle 6: Geographic Arbitrage

**Same product, different countries, different competition.**

From the Q4 Radar results:
- Espresso bundle: Sweden (78.9) > Germany (71.9) > Switzerland (68.5)
- Dog hammock: Switzerland (75.6) > Sweden (69.1) > UK (66.6)
- Ski tuning: Switzerland (74.8) > Sweden (73.3)

**The play:** A product that's saturated in the UK might be underserved in Switzerland. Same product, different Google Shopping landscape.

## Angle 7: The "Do It For Them" Service

**Don't sell the tool. Sell the outcome.**

From the internet asset playbook:
> "recurring demand → structured/verified data → decision tool → commercial action → automated maintenance"

Examples:
- **SaaS Savings Desk** — "We'll find you £5k/year in software savings" (£299)
- **Tender Bid Desk** — "We'll find and triage relevant tenders" (£199/month)
- **RFQ Sourcing Desk** — "We'll get you 3 supplier quotes" (£99)
