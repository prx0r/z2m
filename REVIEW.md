## The Meta-Insight

The winning formula across everything we've researched:

> **Pain × Personalization × Distribution × Moat**

- **Pain** = people actively spending money on something painful
- **Personalization** = AI makes 1:1 customization economically viable
- **Distribution** = Google free listings + Etsy + Pinterest (not paid ads first)
- **Moat** = live data + compatibility + localization + trust

Every successful product we found follows this pattern. Every failed one is missing at least one element.
