# Products for AI-Native Founders

**The modern founder: 1 person + Claude + vibe coding. What do they need?**

---

## The Profile

- Solo founder or tiny team (1-3 people)
- Uses Claude/GPT for everything
- Vibe codes with Cursor/Copilot/Claude Code
- Ships fast, iterates faster
- No budget for hires
- Revenue: $0-50k MRR
- Wants to 10x output without 10x headcount

---

## Product Ideas

### 1. Plug-and-Play Customer Support Agent

**What:** AI chatbot trained on their docs, branded, deploys in 10 minutes

**How it works:**
- Founder uploads: docs, FAQ, product pages, past tickets
- Agent trains on their content
- Embeds as widget on their site
- Escalates to human when needed
- Learns from conversations

**Pricing:** $49/mo (100 conversations), $149/mo (unlimited), $399/mo (multi-brand)

**Why it works:** Every founder needs support but can't hire. This is the first thing they'd pay for.

**Tech:** Claude API + vector store + widget embed. Simple.

### 2. AI-Powered Invoice/Proposal Generator

**What:** Describe the project → get a professional invoice/proposal in 30 seconds

**How it works:**
- Founder describes: "Built a landing page for a SaaS, took 3 days"
- AI generates: line items, pricing, terms, branding
- Sends via email with payment link
- Tracks: sent/opened/paid

**Pricing:** $29/mo (20 docs), $79/mo (unlimited), $199/mo (with accounting sync)

**Why it works:** Every freelancer/founder wastes hours on this. AI makes it instant.

### 3. One-Click Terms & Privacy Policy Generator

**What:** Answer 10 questions → get lawyer-grade ToS + Privacy Policy + Cookie Policy

**How it works:**
- Founder answers: business type, jurisdiction, data collected, cookies used
- AI generates: GDPR-compliant privacy policy, ToS, cookie notice
- Includes: CCPA, UK GDPR, EU GDPR variants
- Auto-updates when laws change

**Pricing:** $49 one-time per document, $99/year for updates

**Why it works:** Every SaaS needs these. Lawyers charge $500-2000. This is $49.

### 4. Competitor Intelligence Bot

**What:** Monitors competitor websites, pricing, job postings, social media

**How it works:**
- Founder adds: 3-5 competitor URLs

- Weekly digest: "Competitor X raised prices 15%, Competitor Y hired 3 engineers, Competitor Z launched feature Z"

**Pricing:** $39/mo (3 competitors), $99/mo (10), $249/mo (unlimited + API)
