## The Model

Not: "Atomic Habits summarized into a 30-page ebook."

Instead: **$19-$79 personalized micro-consultant in a PDF/web page.**

User fills 8-20 questions. System generates:

```
their data + expert source corpus + deterministic calculations + AI synthesis
    ↓
beautiful 20-40 page bespoke report
```

### The Sweet Spot

> "The £40k Kitchen Renovation Pre-Flight Pack — upload your quotes and plans and get your personalized budget, contractor comparison, 63-point checklist, questions to ask each builder and red-flag report. — £39"

**£39 purchase attached to a £40,000 decision.**

## The Personalization Play

Etsy data proves people pay for structured text generation:

- Personalized astrology: 20,100 reviews
- Career astrology reading: 9,800 reviews at $22.49
- Custom manifestation plan: $30, ~395 reviews
- Numerology reports: 1,000-3,000 reviews, $7-30

These are AI-era products. Customer **wants** personalization.

Build transparent branded system. Sell as personalized self-reflection/entertainment.

### Example: Your 2027 Life Blueprint — $39

Inputs: DOB, location, relationship, career, concerns, goals, money, personality, lifestyle

Output: astrology section, personality analysis, goals, habits, 12-month priorities, monthly prompts, relationship reflection, career reflection, "things to stop doing", printable action plan

**You're selling: "I spent 12 minutes answering questions and something spent an hour thinking specifically about me."**

## Target Rich People's Expensive Problems

Formula:

**Customer wealth × cost of underlying decision × anxiety × search intent ÷ sophistication of existing digital products**

---

**London → Dubai Relocation Blueprint — £39**

## The Important Switch

Don't look for: people who buy ebooks.

Look for: **people currently spending £5k-100k who will happily spend another £29 to avoid making a mistake.**

The Etsy astrology/manifestation market proves: **personalized digital text still has significant perceived monetary value in 2026.** AI hasn't destroyed that market — it made production almost free.

**Test first: automated personalized-report factory, dozens of tiny storefronts, narrow high-intent high-disposable-income searches.**
