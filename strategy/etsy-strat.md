# Etsy Strategy — 100 Shops, 100K+ Sales Analysis

Source: YouTube transcript (Sept 2026)
Study: 100 Etsy shops with 100,000+ sales

---

## 6 Proven Strategies

### 1. Run Sales (25%+ Off)
- 93/100 shops running sales
- 84 offering 25%+ off
- 78 applying sale to entire shop
- Median discount: 38.5%
- Creates urgency + shows in "on sale" filter

### 2. Similar Pricing Methods
**Low-price product strategy:**
- 59/100 shops use strategically lower-priced variants to appear cheaper in search
- Example: shirt shows $5.72 but actual price is $20 (different variant)
- Example: sweatshirt shows $27.99 but that's digital file only, actual product is $71-91

**Shipping strategy:**
- 81% charge for shipping (only 19% free)
- 67% charge under $6 (prioritized in search)
- 54% offer free shipping over $35 (boosts order size)
- Median shipping: $5.70
- Etsy prioritizes listings with shipping under $6

### 3. High-Quality Mock-ups
- 91/100 shops have professional photos
- 58% use flatlay mock-ups only
- 10% use model mock-ups only
- 19% use both flatlay + model
- 13% use actual product photos
- Flatlays win on top-selling items

### 4. Sell Multiple Products
- 97% offer 2+ products
- 85% offer 5+ products
- 63% have multiple variations in most-sold listing
- Diversifies revenue, increases sales potential

### 5. 500+ Listings
- 74% have 500+ listings
- 60% have 1,000+ listings
- 29% have 2,000+ listings
- Median: 1,611 listings
- Range: 45 to 7,365

### 6. Maximize Keywords & SEO
- 74% use 130+ characters in title
- 96% use all 13 tags
- 73% separate keywords with comma+space
- 60% match tags to title keywords (though Etsy says this is outdated)
- Focus: clear, descriptive, relevant keywords

---

## Bonus Tips

### Personalization
- 66/100 top sellers have customized/personalized products
- Etsy: 1 in 3 buyers comes specifically for personalized items
- Personalization searches up 43% YoY

### All 10 Photos + Video
- 48/100 use all 10 listing photos
- 91% include size chart
- 80% have additional info cards (shipping, care, etc.)
- 49/100 have listing video

### Thorough Descriptions
- 96/100 have thorough descriptions
- Include: shipping times, materials, care, how to order, returns

### Shop Banner
- 96/100 have banners
- Only 35 promote beyond name/photos
- Best practice: highlight sales, bundles, social proof

### Shop Sections
- 98/100 have organized sections
- Critical for browsing experience

### About Section
- 73/100 have about sections
- Share business story, team, inspiration

### Social Links
- 51/100 link socials/website
- Use Etsy's unique code for fee savings

### Shop Policies
- 97/100 have policies filled out

### FAQs
- 54/100 have FAQs
- Reduces customer service questions

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Volume wins** — 1,000+ listings, 5+ products, 10 photos each
2. **Sales create urgency** — 25%+ off, countdown timers
3. **Pricing is strategic** — low-price variants, shipping under $6
4. **Personalization is huge** — 66% of top sellers customize
5. **SEO matters** — 130+ chars, all 13 tags, long-tail keywords
6. **Professional presentation** — mock-ups, videos, thorough descriptions
