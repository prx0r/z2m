# AI-Native Premium Commerce Strategy

**The core thesis: Build the best AI sales layer for expensive things that other companies already know how to make and fulfil.**

---

## What Changed

Several previously expensive business functions collapsed in cost simultaneously:

- **Creative production** — Higgsfield: 8-sec video $2.38, 15-sec $4.46. Runway: Product Ad API with 10 reference images. Veo 3.1: $0.05-0.60/sec.
- **Research/analysis** — Astra: 72.6% OSWorld, 1M+ token context, computer use, browsing, code
- **Localization** — AI translation + creative adaptation across countries
- **Sales consultation** — AI advisor that understands products, compatibility, room constraints
- **Operations** — Browser automation for wholesaler portals, tracking, returns

## Three Business Modes

### 1. Own Retailers — Highest Margin
Build specialist stores (home cinema, espresso, golf simulator, garden rooms).

### 2. Lead/Broker Sites — Lowest Capital
Upload → render → qualify → sell lead to partner contractor.

### 3. Sell the Stack — Immediate Service Cashflow
Take existing £5m/year retailer. Install: canonical product graph, compatibility data, FAQ attributes, AI shopping assistant, UCP integration, creative-generation pipeline.

## 100 Hypotheses Per SKU

Don't make one product video. Make 100 positions:

```
PRODUCT: £1,499 launch monitor

AUDIENCE A: "Build a simulator in your garage"
AUDIENCE B: "Practice properly through winter"
AUDIENCE C: "Turn your spare room into a golf studio"
AUDIENCE D: "Know exactly why you're losing distance"
AUDIENCE E: "Premium home golf without £20k installation"

3 hooks × 3 videos × 3 images × 2 landing pages = 90 positions
```

Let performance data decide. AI generates, deploys, measures, reasons, regenerates.

## The Salesperson Becomes Multimodal

Not: "Hi! How may I assist?"

Instead:

> Customer uploads room photo.
> Advisor: "Based on your 4.8 × 3.7m room, I'd use the short-throw projector."
> Generates: their room with equipment installed.
> "Want the £3,800 configuration or the £5,400 version?"
> Dynamically constructs the bundle.

## Product Pages Become Generated Experiences

Search: `best launch monitor small garage UK`

Don't dump on `/products/launch-monitor`. Give them:

# Build a golf simulator for a small UK garage

> Ceiling height? Garage depth? Right/left handed? Budget?

Products reorganize dynamically. Google is shifting toward conversational queries.

## B2B Procurement

Astra is trained for spreadsheets, CAD, professional workflows.

> "Design equipment package for a 60-cover pizza restaurant."

AI ingests floor plan → oven, refrigeration, prep, dishwashing, storage, ventilation, BOM, supplier options, quote deck.

Same for: workshops, dental practices, gyms, restaurants.

## After-Sale Operations

Customer: "The grinder arrived but the machine hasn't."

Astra checks: supplier portal A, supplier portal B, email, tracking. Then replies.

Returns: checks policy, creates RMA, contacts distributor, generates shipping instructions, updates CRM.

Computer-use capability means browser interfaces are automatable. Wholesalers are often technologically prehistoric.
