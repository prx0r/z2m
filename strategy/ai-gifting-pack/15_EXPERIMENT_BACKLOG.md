## Scoring each test

Record:
- hypothesis;
- expected mechanism;
- sample size;
- cost;
- primary metric;
- guardrail;
- result;
- next action.

Never label a design “winner” because it got likes.
A winner ultimately produces contribution or high-value lifecycle behavior.
