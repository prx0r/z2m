## The Thesis (Verdict: STRONG)

> Build a recipient/occasion-aware gift creation engine that turns a few facts + one or more photos into genuinely good, print-ready physical gifts in seconds, then fulfils locally.

**Key insight:** "Sell the finished emotional result, not the customiser."

The consumer should not feel like they're using Photoshop, Canva, or a prompt box. They should answer 5-6 questions and get 4-8 finished, purchasable concepts.

## Key Insights

### 1. The Emotional Result, Not the Customiser

Moonpig sells cards. We sell "the feeling that something thought specifically about me."

The difference:
- Moonpig: pick a design, add text, buy
- Us: describe the person, get 4-8 finished concepts, buy

### 2. Recipient Profiles Are the Real Moat

If a customer creates a profile for "mum" once, every future birthday/Christmas is a one-click purchase. Moonpig's 101M reminders/year prove this works.

### 3. Amazon's Entry Validates but Doesn't Kill

Amazon launched AI custom merch in June 2026. This validates the primitive but kills "just call an image API." Compete on:
- Emotional depth (not just surface customization)
- Curated collections (not search results)
- Local fulfilment (not 2-week AliExpress)
- Gift-specific UX (not generic POD)

### 4. Cards Are Acquire But Don't Monetise

£3-5 cards can't carry paid CAC. Use them as:
- Acquisition channel
- Reminder capture
- Cross-sell to higher-AOV products
- Subscription/membership driver

### 5. The 40 Experiments Are Gold

The experiment backlog (40 tests across UX, creative, product, recipient, offer) is exactly what the finalbuilds2 hypothesis system needs.
