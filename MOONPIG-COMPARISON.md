# Us vs Moonpig — The Comparison

---

## Moonpig (The Incumbent)

**Revenue:** £373M FY26 (€0.43B)
**Customers:** 12.3M active
**EBITDA:** £87.2M (23.4% margin)
**Orders:** 36M (Moonpig + Greetz)
**Reminders:** 113M occasion reminders
**Memberships:** 1.2M Moonpig Plus / Greetz Plus
**Repeat rate:** 89.4% revenue from existing customers

### What Moonpig Does Well
- Brand recognition (FTSE 250)
- 113M occasion reminders (the moat)
- 40,000+ card designs
- Fast fulfilment (print + ship same day)
- Data: collects 2× more data than rest of greeting card market combined
- AI: "enhancing effectiveness through greater personalisation and deeper understanding of customer relationships"

### What Moonpig Doesn't Do
- **Physical gifts beyond cards/flowers/chocolate** (limited range)
- **AI-generated content** (they recommend, they don't create)
- **Pet-specific personalization** (not a vertical)
- **Multi-product from one input** (one card, not a collection)
- **Persistent recipient profiles** across product types
- **Visualized outcome** (they show products, not your result)

## The Key Difference

**Moonpig:** "Here are 40,000 cards. Pick one."

**Us:** "Tell us about Luna. Here are 8 products we made specifically for her."

Moonpig's customer journey:
1. Search "birthday card for mum"
2. Browse 40,000 designs
3. Pick one
4. Add text
5. Buy

Our customer journey:
1. Upload photo of Luna
2. Answer 5 questions about Luna
3. AI generates 8 products for Luna
4. Pick favourite
5. Buy bundle

**The AI advisor IS the product. The physical items are the output.**

## The Attack Strategy

**Don't compete on Moonpig's territory (cards).**

**Compete on territory Moonpig doesn't own:**

1. **Multi-product personalization** — one input → entire collection
2. **Pet vertical** — dedicated, AI-powered, emotional
3. **Physical gifts beyond cards** — ornaments, books, mugs, calendars
4. **Persistent recipient profiles** — the gift that remembers
5. **AI advisor experience** — "make something for them" not "pick from a list"
