## Etsy SEO Rules (2026)

- Clear titles under 15 words
- Move generic phrases ("gift for him") to tags
- Use specific product + traits in title
- Tags: recipient + occasion + material + style

**Bad:** "Personalized Gift for Mum Custom Family Cookbook Christmas Gift"
**Good:** "Personalized Family Recipe Cookbook From Your Photos and Recipes"
